## §0 — ORIENTATION

### §0.1 What This File Is

This is the **unified mechanism framework** — a complete account of HOW
body-based therapies produce their therapeutic effects, from the moment a
stimulus contacts the body surface to the emergence of lasting change.

Human-Predictive-Drive Core-Software describes HOW the brain PROCESSES signals ("how it runs").
This file describes HOW therapy signals TRAVEL, TRANSFORM, and PRODUCE EFFECTS
through body infrastructure ("how therapy works").

Together with Core-Body-Architecture (the hardware), this file completes the
Body-Signal-Architecture:


### §0.4 The Central Architectural Finding

Body therapy does not work through ONE pathway. It works through SIMULTANEOUS
activation of MULTIPLE systems across MULTIPLE processing depths on MULTIPLE
timescales, with context modulating every stage. The therapeutic effect is the
INTEGRATION of this multi-dimensional process — not any single mechanism. 🟡

This finding has a practical consequence: no single-mechanism explanation
("it works because of endorphins" / "it's just placebo") can be correct.
Every such claim captures at most one dimension of a multi-dimensional process.

### §1.2 The Answer

A physical stimulus activates the body's 9-system signaling network
(Core-Body-Architecture) simultaneously. Signals travel through PARALLEL
streams at different speeds (milliseconds to months) and different depths
(local tissue to cortex). Many signals NEVER reach the brain — they produce
therapeutic effects within the body's own processing infrastructure (Levels
0-2). Those that do reach the brain are modulated by Evaluative Gates that
can amplify or suppress the signal based on context. Over repeated sessions,
two independent acceleration mechanisms — gate compilation and interoceptive
improvement — multiply the therapeutic effect. 🟡

### §1.3 Three Key Implications

**Implication 1: No single-mechanism explanation suffices.** A therapy
activating 5-7 systems simultaneously across 5 processing depths on 6
timescales cannot be explained by "endorphins" or "placebo" alone. Both
capture real dimensions but neither captures the architecture. 🟡

**Implication 2: The non-specific floor is NOT "just placebo."** The floor
(~SMD 0.2-0.4) has TWO components: Direct-State hardware (~SMD 0.1-0.2:
gate control, parasympathetic shift, basic touch comfort) + Evaluative
Gates minimum (~SMD 0.1-0.2: encounter context, narrative, attention
investment). Both are REAL physiological processes — the first is body
hardware, the second is brain processing. Neither is illusory. 🟡

**Implication 3: Body therapy uniquely accesses BOTH the Hardware Economy
(Direct-State, near-universal, low variation) AND the Software Economy
(Evaluative, culture-dependent, high variation) simultaneously.** Cognitive
therapy accesses software only. Pharmacotherapy accesses hardware only.
Body therapy accesses both — this is its architectural distinction. 🟡

### §2.4 The Confidence Gradient

Mechanism confidence follows a spatial pattern:

```
L1-L2 (local/spinal):       🟢 Well-measured. Direct observation,
                             electrophysiology, molecular biology.
                             Gate control, axon reflex, PROKR2.

L3-L4 (supraspinal/cortical): 🟡 Moderate. fMRI, animal models,
                               pharmacological challenge. Descending
                               modulation, GABA, neuroplasticity.

L5-L6 (systemic/cognitive):  🔴 Weakest. Hardest to isolate, most
                             confounded. Cortisol (Moyer caveat),
                             self-efficacy, scaffolding contribution.
```

**The confidence paradox:** the MOST clinically relevant mechanisms
(L5-L6 sustained change: neuroplasticity, self-efficacy, gate
compilation) have the LOWEST research confidence; the BEST-understood
mechanisms (L1-L2: gate control, axon reflex) are often the least
clinically relevant (transient effects only). 🟢

frequency-dependent opioid modulation (Han: 2Hz = enkephalin,
100Hz = dynorphin) to the underlying stimulus. 🟢

#### §3.1.2 Three Stimulus Principles

**Force DIRECTION > force MAGNITUDE.** The body responds to HOW
force is applied, not just how much:

```
DIRECTION         EXAMPLE           MECHANISM
──────────────── ───────────────── ─────────────────────────
Perpendicular ↓   Pressure (B02-04) Gate control → endocannabinoid system cascade
Penetration ↓↓    Needle (B01,B11)  Micro-wound → adenosine 24×
Decompression ↑   Suction (B07)     Capillary distension → HO-1
Lateral →         Shear (B09)       Inter-layer sliding + HO-1
Rapid thrust →→   HVLA (B10)        Proprioceptive burst (<200ms)
```

B10 (thrust, 100-600N) and B04 (compression, 40-110N) have comparable
force MAGNITUDES but completely different activation fingerprints
because DIRECTION differs. 🟡

**Pressure = PATHWAY SELECTOR.** Increasing pressure doesn't just
increase dose — it switches which systems activate and which reward
type is produced (D03 §5.2):

```
PRESSURE  PATHWAY ACTIVATED              REWARD TYPE         TIMESCALE
───────── ──────────────────────────── ──────────────────── ──────────
<5N       CT afferents + oxytocin      Direct-State Touch   20-100ms
5-40N     Gate control + somatovisc.   Direct-State Relief / E₀      100-300ms
40-65N    endocannabinoid system activation (CB1/CB2)     Direct-State endocannabinoid system     minutes
65-110N   Descending modulation (PAG)  E₁ Evaluative        200ms-1s
>110N     Full cascade all layers      All simultaneously   all
```

Light massage = CT-mediated PLEASURE (Direct-State). Deep massage
= PAG-mediated PAIN MODULATION (Evaluative, opioid). These are
DIFFERENT THINGS, not more or less of the same thing. The endocannabinoid system
threshold at ~40-65N is the most clinically important transition
point — below it, no endocannabinoid response; above it, AEA ↑168%
(McPartland 2005). Molecular substrate: CB1/CB2 in fascial fibroblasts
(Fede 2016). Threshold NOT yet directly measured. 🟡

**Self-generated movement = configurable stimulus.** B15/B16 are
the ONLY therapies where the patient IS the stimulus source, and the
activation profile is user-configurable by style selection. Yoga's
Adaptive Spectrum: Yin = A03 fascial, Restorative = A02 autonomic,
Vinyasa = A04+A08 cardiovascular, Kundalini = A02+A06 neurochemical.
No passive therapy offers this configurability. 🟡

### §3.2 Signal Transduction

Once stimulus is received, signals travel through Human-Predictive-Drive's Gap 2 — the
"driver layer" between body (Layer 1) and brain processing (Layer 3).
Body-Signal-Architecture reveals this is NOT a serial pipeline but PARALLEL STREAMS through
independent channels at different speeds (D01).

#### §3.2.1 Gap 2 = Two Halves

```
                    Body-Signal-Architecture MAPS                    Human-Predictive-Drive MAPS
                    (body-side)                 (brain-side)
                         │                          │
BODY SURFACE ────► Receptor→Signal (SG-2.1) ◄── 60-70% covered
                   Signal→Routing (SG-2.2)  ◄── 40-50%
                   ─────── BOUNDARY ≈ THALAMUS/BRAINSTEM ───────
                   Cortical→Pattern (SG-2.3) ►── 5-10% (frontier)
                   NT→Function (SG-2.4)     ◄── 50-60%
                   Plasticity→Compile (SG-2.5)►── 5-10%
                   Sleep Consolidation (SG-2.6)►── 0% (Body-Signal-Architecture scope)
                                                    │
                                              ► Human-Predictive-Drive TERRITORY
```

Body-Signal-Architecture fills the BODY-SIDE sub-gaps (SG-2.1, 2.2, 2.4) but not the
BRAIN-SIDE (SG-2.3, 2.5, 2.6). The bridge between body and brain
is "half-built from the body side up." The handoff point is the
posterior insula — where Body-Signal-Architecture's signals arrive and Human-Predictive-Drive's processing
begins (Craig 2002, 2009). 🟡

### §3.4 Evaluative Modulation

Every therapeutic signal — regardless of its depth in the Reward
Gradient — is modulated by Evaluative Gates before becoming conscious
experience. This modulation can amplify, suppress, or transform the
signal (D04).

#### §3.4.1 The 5-Step Insula Gradient

The gate mechanism operates through a 5-step process in the insula
cortex (D04 §2.1):

```
STEP 1  POSTERIOR INSULA (0-50ms)
        Direct-State signal arrives RAW — same regardless of
        who/why/where. Craig 2002: primary interoceptive cortex.

STEP 2  EVALUATIVE ASSESSMENT (50-200ms)
        Parallel top-down context: WHO is doing this? WHAT is
        expected? WHERE am I? Amygdala fast path ~12ms for threat.

STEP 3  ANTERIOR INSULA: INTEGRATION (100-300ms)
        Direct-State signal + evaluative context → EXPERIENCED signal.
        The gate operates HERE — same Direct-State input produces
        different experiences depending on context.

STEP 4  ACC: CONFLICT CHECK (200-400ms)
        Direct-State positive + Eval positive = silent (no conflict).
        Direct-State positive + Eval negative = FIRES (suppress).
        Direct-State negative + Eval positive = FIRES (placebo).
        Botvinick 2004: ACC conflict monitoring.

STEP 5  OFC/vmPFC: VALUE ASSIGNMENT (300-500ms)
        Final value tag. Damasio 1994: somatic marker.
```

**Four gate outcomes** + one Body-Signal-Architecture-discovered:

- **AMPLIFY:** Direct-State + positive Eval → enhanced experience (trusted
  massage, skilled acupuncture)
- **SUPPRESS:** Direct-State overridden by negative Eval → diminished
  experience (unexpected pain, distrust)
- **ABSENT:** Raw Direct-State, no Eval modulation → infant, PFC offline,
  dissociation
- **CONFLICTED:** Ambiguous signal → arousal + caution, ACC fires
  strongly
- **LAYERED** (new, 🔴): Simultaneous DIFFERENT gate settings at
  different Reward Gradient depths. Example: B04 deep tissue —
  surface CT = AMPLIFY, deep fascial manipulation = CONFLICTED,
  net experience = "hurts good." De qi (B01): sensation-level =
  CONFLICTED (heavy/aching) + framework-level = AMPLIFY (qi
  arriving) = LAYERED.

#### §3.4.6 Session Design = Gate Management

The gate mechanism has direct clinical implications (D04 §5.3):

```
PHASE 1: GATE ESTABLISHMENT (first 5-10 min)
  Warm room, calm voice, consent, gentle contact.
  DO NOT introduce dissonance-producing techniques yet.

PHASE 2: GRADUATED TECHNIQUE (session body)
  Progressive intensity. Verbal warnings before changes.
  Monitor for gate SHIFT (tension, withdrawal, verbal cues).
  If detected: reduce intensity BEFORE SUPPRESS locks in.

PHASE 3: GATE REINFORCEMENT (final 5-10 min)
  Return to gentle techniques. Verbal affirmation.
  Positive closing compiles positive history for next session.
```

**The negativity ratio:** One moment of unexpected pain can undo 10
minutes of positive gate-setting (~10:1 ratio, derived from loss
aversion ~2× amplified by cortisol persistence). Investment in
PREVENTING negative moments yields higher return than investment in
CREATING positive ones. 🟡

**Asymmetric transition timing:** Reward→Dissonance is FAST (amygdala
~12ms, NE spike ~500ms, opioid deactivation seconds, cortisol arrives
5-20min then sustains 60-90min). Dissonance→Reward is SLOW (cortisol
must clear, NE must drop, opioid re-activation requires all 5 Body-
Feedback-Preconditions to re-verify). Design implication: protect the
positive gate setting above all else. 🟢

dissociation, or low-body-awareness populations.

a GATEWAY to interoceptive development for populations who cannot
directly access visceral awareness. 🟡

#### §3.5.6 The Double Virtuous Cycle

across sessions). Together they create a DOUBLE virtuous cycle
(D05 §4.4):

```
Session N:
  Gate compilation (stronger AMPLIFY) +
  Interoception improvement (better reading) →
  Direct-State signal both AMPLIFIED (D04) and BETTER READ (D05) →
  Outcome MULTIPLICATIVELY improved

Session N+1:
  Better Direct-State signal → more accurate prediction (D04) +
  More accurate reading → more precise body-confirm (D05/SAL) →
  Both cycles reinforce each other
```

This explains two puzzling clinical observations:

1. **Outcomes often ACCELERATE after first few sessions** — two

2. **First-session outcomes are POOR predictors of long-term
   success** — neither compilation process has had time to develop.
   Dropping patients after 1-2 sessions of "no response" may be
   premature if the acceleration mechanisms have not yet engaged. 🔴

## §5 — EMERGENT ARCHITECTURE

These principles emerge ONLY from integrating all 35 source files through
the lens of the 5-stage therapeutic process. No individual file contains
them; they are properties of the architecture as a whole.

### §5.1 The Multi-Dimensional Process Principle

Body therapy does not work through ONE mechanism. A single B01 needle
simultaneously activates gate control (ms), axon reflex (s), adenosine
(min), somatovisceral (s), autonomic shift (min), endocannabinoid system if deep (min),
Han opioid if EA (min), immune response (hr), neuroplastic (wk) — nine
parallel streams on independent timescales. Dose-response is therefore
NOT one curve but MULTIPLE simultaneous curves, each with different onset,
peak, and decay.

Any explanation of the form "therapy X works because of Y" captures at
most one dimension. This includes "endorphins," "fascia release," "energy
flow," AND "placebo." All are partial. 🟡

### §5.2 The Dual Economy

Body therapy uniquely accesses TWO simultaneous reward economies:

- **Hardware Economy** (Direct-State): stimulus → receptor → body-state.
  Near-universal, low variation, expectation-irrelevant. The FLOOR
  (~SMD 0.2-0.4) that no patient drops below.
- **Software Economy** (Evaluative): compiled schemas → evaluation →
  modulated experience. High variation, culture-dependent. The CEILING
  (~15-95% of total effect) that varies by therapy and patient.

Cognitive therapy accesses software only. Pharmacotherapy accesses
hardware only. Body therapy accesses BOTH — this is its architectural
distinction and explains its unusually broad clinical range (8 condition
domains for B01, 7 for B16). 🟡

### §5.3 The Mechanism Inequality Principle

51 mechanisms follow a power law: 7 universal (14%) are shared by most
therapies; 15 unique (29%) define individual therapy identity. The BASE
therapeutic effect is SIMILAR across therapies; differentiation comes
from the TAIL.

**Consequence:** Chronic pain responds to 12/16 therapies because pain
engages all 7 universal mechanisms. Head-to-head trials between therapies
often show small/NS differences because the shared base dominates. The
sham paradox dissolves: sham activates universal mechanisms too — the
specific layer (SMD 0.1-0.3) requires large N to detect. 🟡

### §5.4 The Confidence Paradox

L1-L2 mechanisms (gate control, axon reflex) are 🟢 well-understood but
clinically transient. L5-L6 mechanisms (neuroplasticity, self-efficacy,
gate compilation) are 🔴 poorly understood but clinically LASTING.

The most important therapeutic changes are the ones we understand least.
This is not an accident — sustained change requires central neural
adaptation, which is harder to study than local tissue response. 🟡

### §5.5 The Double Virtuous Cycle

Gate compilation (D04: stronger AMPLIFY gates across sessions) ×
interoception improvement (D05: better signal reading across sessions)
= therapy outcomes ACCELERATE after first sessions, not plateau.

This explains two puzzling clinical observations: (1) outcomes often
improve nonlinearly after sessions 3-5; (2) first-session outcomes are
POOR predictors of long-term success. Both cycles need time to engage.
Dropping patients after 1-2 sessions of "no response" may be premature
if the acceleration mechanisms have not yet developed. 🔴

### §5.6 Scaffolding as Prediction Generator

Therapeutic scaffolding (TCM meridians, zone maps, energy flow) functions
as a PREDICTION GENERATOR: scaffolding → patient prediction → Direct-State signals
confirm → positive prediction error → Evaluative Gates AMPLIFY → enhanced
experience → stronger prediction next time.

The scaffolding's TRUTH is irrelevant to this function. It needs to be
BELIEVED, not CORRECT. This is why different scaffolding systems produce
SIMILAR non-specific outcomes — same function, different content.

The Scaffolding Paradox: dismantling false scaffolding may REDUCE
therapeutic effect (~0.2-0.4 SMD, C03 P2) but maintaining it perpetuates
misconceptions. Resolution: can we provide equally compelling ACCURATE
frameworks? Body-Signal-Architecture itself attempts this. 🟡

### §5.7 Active-Passive as Architectural Distinction

Active (B15/B16) and passive (B01-B14) therapies are not quality levels
but two DIFFERENT Zone D engagement strategies:

- Passive = Zone D INJECTION (external input, no skill transfer, acute)
- Active = Zone D Bridge TRAINING (self-generated, cumulative, chronic)

This explains the exclusive domain split: fall prevention, T2DM,
Parkinson's balance respond ONLY to active (capacity-building required);
PONV, acute pain, trigger point respond primarily to passive (targeted input
required). Neither strategy is superior — they serve different
architectural needs. 🟡
