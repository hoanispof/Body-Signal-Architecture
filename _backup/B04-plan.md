### Evidence Landscape
```
DEEP TISSUE / MFR EVIDENCE:
  Systematic reviews:
    Deep tissue massage:       Arsovski 2025 RCT (n=150, 8 weeks, athletes)
                               Multiple sport massage meta-analyses (recovery,
                               DOMS, CK reduction)
    Myofascial release:        Lu 2024 (headache: TTH, cervicogenic, migraine)
                               2023 meta-analysis (chronic neck pain: 10 RCTs,
                                 VAS significant, PPT/ROM inconclusive)
                               2023 SR (chronic LBP: 6 studies, isolated MFR)

                               Mixed vs control: some insignificant

                               Ernst 2004 review: "severely limited evidence"

                               ↑pain tolerance vs inactive, no self-reported

KEY EVIDENCE PROBLEM:
  ① No standardized definition of "deep tissue massage" — protocols vary
     enormously between practitioners and studies
  ② Blinding extremely difficult — deep pressure is subjectively obvious
  ③ Rolfing/SI has near-zero controlled evidence despite 70+ year history
  ④ Most RCTs combine deep tissue with other interventions
  ⑤ Short-term follow-up dominant — long-term effects unestablished
  ⑥ Fascial structural changes assumed but rarely verified with imaging
     (Gao 2026: "changes in pain/ROM do not verify altered fascial
      stiffness, hydration, adhesion, or inter-layer glide")
```

### Central Question
```
THE FASCIAL REMODELING HYPOTHESIS — DOES DEEP TISSUE WORK
PRODUCE STRUCTURAL CHANGE OR JUST NEURAL MODULATION?

  B04's CORE CLAIM:
    → Deep tissue techniques produce STRUCTURAL TISSUE CHANGES:
      fascial elongation, adhesion disruption, collagen remodeling,
      HA viscosity restoration, trigger point deactivation
    → These structural changes explain why effects differ from and
      exceed standard massage (B03 Level 3)

  THE CHALLENGE:
    → Crane 2012 proves mechanotransduction at molecular level ✓
    → BUT: Gao 2026 notes "changes in pain/ROM do not verify altered
      fascial stiffness, hydration, adhesion, or inter-layer glide"
    → A08 muscle stiffness paradox: mechanical effects return within
      MINUTES, but pain relief lasts hours-days
    → = If structural change is transient, then the PERSISTENT effects
      must be NEURAL (descending modulation, central sensitization
      reversal, autonomic shift)
    → = B04 may be primarily a NEURAL intervention that happens to
      work through deep mechanical stimulation, rather than a
      STRUCTURAL intervention as traditionally claimed

  CONTRAST WITH B03 (General Massage):
    → B03 Level 3 (moderate) = "sweet spot" for most clinical uses
    → B03 Level 4 (deep) = B04 territory = different mechanism profile
    → KEY DISTINCTION: B04 = endocannabinoid-dominant, descending
      modulation-driven. B03 standard = oxytocin-dominant, CT-driven.
    → B04 adds FASCIAL mechanotransduction as primary target

  THE endocannabinoid system THRESHOLD:
    → Lindgren 2015: gentle touch = NO endocannabinoid system change
    → McPartland 2005: deep OMT = AEA ↑168%
    → Fede 2016: CB1+CB2 receptors present in fascial tissue
    → = Deep mechanical loading of fascia activates endocannabinoid
      system via fascial CB receptors — a PRESSURE-DEPENDENT
      THRESHOLD MECHANISM unique to B04-level work

  FRAMEWORK PREDICTION:
    B04 = Level 4 of B03's Pressure-Dependent Pathway Model
    = The point where mechanical input crosses from
      "systemic regulation" (B03) to "structural targeting" (B04)
    = BOTH are real — but the question is which produces the
      clinically relevant lasting effects
```

### Boundary Decisions
```
B04 vs B03 (Massage-General):
  B03 = general principles + light-to-moderate pressure + multi-tradition
  B04 = deep tissue techniques + fascial targeting + structural intent
  B03 §7.2 boundary table already defines the overlap zone
  B04 EXTENDS B03 Level 4 — does NOT repeat it. Cross-references §5.3.
  INTENT matters: systemic relaxation = B03; structural change = B04

B04 vs B11 (Dry Needling):
  BOTH target trigger points
  B11 = INVASIVE (needle → LTR → ACh depletion)
  B04 = NON-INVASIVE (manual compression → ischemic → reactive hyperemia)
  B11 has LTR mechanism that B04 lacks
  B04 has broader fascial access that B11's point-targeting misses

B04 vs B07 (Cupping):
  B07 = NEGATIVE pressure (decompression → lifts fascial layers)
  B04 = POSITIVE pressure (compression → pushes through fascial layers)
  Opposite mechanical vectors → different tissue responses
  Both can target fascia but via inverted mechanisms

B04 vs B09 (Gua Sha — not yet written):
  B09 = SHEAR force across superficial fascia
  B04 = COMPRESSION force into deep fascia
  Different force vectors, both target fascia
  Both produce localized tissue response + HO-1 activation

B04 vs B01 (Acupuncture):
  Needle grasp (Langevin) activates same fibroblast pathways
  B04 accesses fascia via sustained manual pressure, not needle
  B01 has PROKR2 specificity that B04 cannot access (no penetration)
  B04 has broader fascial field coverage per stroke
```
