### Central Question
```
NEGATIVE PRESSURE (decompression) — the UNIQUE stimulus:

  Every other Track B therapy applies POSITIVE FORCE to tissue:
    → Acupuncture: needle penetration (compression + shear)
    → Massage: compression + friction
    → Acupressure: sustained compression
    → Gua sha: scraping (shear force)
    → Dry needling: needle penetration

  Cupping ALONE applies NEGATIVE PRESSURE (suction):
    → Tissue is LIFTED and DECOMPRESSED
    → Fascial layers SEPARATED (opposite of compressed)
    → Blood drawn INTO tissue (opposite of pushed through)
    → Capillaries DISTENDED from outside (not compressed)

  This creates a UNIQUE mechanism profile:
    → L1 Vascular: PRIMARY (HO-1 pathway, A04 §3.5)
    → L2 Fascial: STRONG (decompression, adhesion release, A03 §4)
    → L3 Neural: MODERATE (gate control Aβ + axon reflex, A01 §4.1)
    → L4 Neuroimmune: MODERATE (controlled tissue damage → immune reset, A05)
    → L5 Endocrine: SECONDARY (CO gasotransmitter, β-endorphin, A06)

  Additional unique dimensions:
    → Wet cupping (hijama) = ONLY body therapy involving bloodletting
    → Marks = THE MECHANISM, not a side effect (A04 §3.5)
    → Oldest documented therapy (1550 BCE) — predates acupuncture
    → Olympic visibility (Phelps 2016) → modern surge
    → Islamic religious endorsement → distinct research tradition
```
