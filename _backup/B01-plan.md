## SESSION PLAN

### Session 2: FOUNDATION (§1-§3)

**§1 — Definition & Origin**
- What acupuncture IS (physical description)
- Historical timeline: Bian Shi → Neijing/Lingshu (200 BCE) → Nan Jing → Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing (260 CE) → Song bronze figures (1027) → Zhenjiu Dacheng (1601) → modern WHO standardization (1984/1991/2008)
- UNESCO recognition 2010
- Traditional theoretical framework AS DATA:
  - Qi concept (what practitioners believe, NOT endorsed as mechanism)
  - Meridian system (12 regular + 8 extraordinary + collaterals)
  - Yin-Yang, Five Elements, Zang-Fu organ theory
  - Pattern differentiation frameworks (Eight Principles, Zang-Fu, Six Stages, Four Levels, San Jiao, Qi-Blood-Fluid)
- 361 WHO standard acupoints + extra points + Ashi points

**§2 — Stimulation Type**
- Physical stimulus: needle insertion through skin into various tissue depths
- Needle specifications: stainless steel, single-use, 0.12-0.30mm diameter, 13-75mm length
- Insertion methods: freehand (Chinese) vs guide tube/shinkan (Japanese, Sugiyama 1694)
- Manipulation techniques: lifting-thrusting (Ti-Cha), twirling (Nian-Zhuan)
- Tonification (Bu) vs Sedation (Xie) techniques
- De Qi sensation: neurological basis (A-delta + C fibers), practitioner "fish bite" sensation
- Electroacupuncture parameters:
  - Frequency: 2Hz / 15Hz / 60Hz / 100Hz / 2-100Hz alternating (dense-disperse)
  - Intensity: 0.5-3.0 mA (low vs high → different pathways, Liu 2020)
  - Waveform: continuous, dense-disperse, intermittent
  - Han's frequency-peptide map (reference A06, add EA-specific detail)
  - HANS device, ITO ES-130, common devices
- Laser acupuncture: wavelengths (632.8nm red, 780-860nm NIR), power (0.5-200mW), non-invasive
- Treatment dose: session duration (20-30 min), frequency (1-3x/week), course (6-12 sessions)

**§3 — Application Points & Areas**
- Point categories:
  - Five Shu/Transport Points (Jing-Well → He-Sea, mother-child logic)
  - Yuan-Source Points (12, constitutional energy)
  - Luo-Connecting Points (15, paired channel treatment)
  - Xi-Cleft Points (16, acute conditions)
  - Back-Shu Points (12, organ-specific, BL channel)
  - Front-Mu Points (12, organ-specific, multi-channel)
  - Eight Confluent Points (extraordinary vessel gateways)
  - Hui-Meeting Points (8 tissue types)
- Point selection logic: local, distal, adjacent, symptomatic, Zang-Fu, mother-child
- Microsystems: auricular (Nogier inverted fetus, NADA 5-point), scalp (Jiao/YNSA/Zhu), hand (KHT)
- Point location methods: proportional cun measurement, anatomical landmarks, palpation, electrical detection

### Session 3: CLINICAL EVIDENCE + EVIDENCE QUALITY (§4, §9)

**§4 — Documented Clinical Effects**
Organized by evidence strength:

🟢 **Strong evidence (multiple positive meta-analyses, guideline recommendations):**
- Chronic low back pain (ACP 2017 first-line, NICE 2021, Vickers ATC)
- Knee osteoarthritis (ACR 2019 conditional recommendation)
- Migraine prevention (Cochrane Linde 2016: NNT=4 vs no treatment, NNT=11 vs sham)
- Tension-type headache (Cochrane: NNT=3 vs routine care)
- PONV (Cochrane 2009: P6 point, 26 trials)
- CINV (38 RCTs, 2,503 patients)
- Postoperative pain (opioid-sparing: 21-29% reduction)

🟡 **Moderate evidence:**
- Chronic neck pain (18 RCTs, durable 3-6 month effect)
- Shoulder pain
- Fibromyalgia (13 SRs, superior to sham, low-moderate quality)
- Depression (64 studies, 7,104 participants — adjunctive)
- Anxiety (SMD -0.41)
- Insomnia (positive meta-analysis 2025)
- Allergic rhinitis (superior to sham, multiple methods)
- Cancer pain (21 RCTs, 1,432 patients)
- Dental pain (WHO 2003 proven)
- Stroke rehabilitation (3,645 RCTs mapped 2015-2024)
- Chronic spontaneous urticaria (22 RCTs, 1,867 patients)

🔴 **Preliminary/emerging:**
- PTSD (VA research active)
- Substance abuse (NADA protocol — mixed RCT evidence despite wide adoption)
- IVF/fertility (modest anxiety relief, no difference vs sham for pregnancy)
- Hypertension, obesity, COPD (GOLD 2021 acknowledged), tinnitus

**§9 — Evidence Assessment**
- Overall quality: Birch 2025 evidence map (10 positive, 82 potential positive, 86 insufficient, 6 no effect)
- Cochrane quality: 16% high, 32% moderate, 52% low (AMSTAR 2)
- **Sham debate 2025 paradigm shift:**
  - Types of sham: Streitberger/Park non-penetrating, needling at non-points, superficial needling, mock laser
  - Kim/Birch/Alraek 2025: "sham needs to be revisited beyond placebo/nocebo"
  - Penetrating sham effect size 0.17 SD vs non-penetrating 0.43 SD (Vickers 2014) — sham is NOT inert
  - Network meta-analysis 2025: differential placebo effects by sham type
  - Sham accounts for ~18.9% of acupuncture's total effect; 40%+ null results flip when corrected
  - Implication: Cochrane reviews systematically UNDERESTIMATE real effects
- **Publication bias:**
  - Vickers 1998: Chinese trials uniformly positive
  - 838/840 Chinese studies (99.8%) positive
  - Wu 2009: only 6.8% of Chinese "RCTs" truly randomized
  - 85.6% of Chinese authors misunderstood randomization
  - ATC approach: IPD meta-analysis with strict allocation concealment → filters out poorly randomized studies
  - When restricted to high-quality Western trials: acupuncture still > sham (0.15-0.23 SD, p<.001)
  - Geographic bias: China ~28% global trials, near-100% positive rate
- **Placebo vs specific effects:**
  - Colquhoun & Novella 2013: "theatrical placebo"
  - Counter: Goldman 2010 (24-fold adenosine, absent in A1-KO mice — animal model, no placebo possible)
  - Kaptchuk 2008 IBS components: waitlist 27% → sham 43% → sham+engagement 62%
  - Vase 2013: expectations account for up to 69.8% of pain variance
  - ATC conclusion: acupuncture > sham (0.15-0.23 SD) — "more than placebo" but modest
  - Wechsler NEJM (asthma): sham = subjective relief only, no objective lung function change
  - Vickers response: effects persist at 12 months (placebo typically decays faster)
- Safety comparison: 0.05 serious events/10,000 treatments vs NSAIDs (5,000-16,500 GI deaths/year US)

### Session 4: MECHANISMS + VARIANTS + SYNTHESIS (§5-§8, §10-§11)

**§5 — Proposed Mechanisms**
- §5.1 Traditional explanation (AS DATA): Qi flow restoration, meridian unblocking, Yin-Yang balance
- §5.2 Modern mechanisms (reference Track A, add acupuncture-specific):
  - Integrated 6-level model:
    - L1 Local: needle → collagen winding → TRPV2/Piezo → mast cell → ATP → adenosine A1
    - L2 Peripheral nerve: A-beta/A-delta/PROKR2+ → DRG
    - L3 Spinal: gate control (LTM → inhibit WDR, r=-0.64), GABAergic
    - L4 Brainstem: PAG→RVM off-cells, NTS→DMV vagal, LC noradrenergic, 5-HT NRM
    - L5 Cortical: DMN modulation, BDNF/TrkB neuroplasticity
    - L6 Systemic: vagal-adrenal catecholamines, M1→M2, NF-kB inhibition, IL-10↑
  - EA-specific: frequency-dependent opioid release (Han complete map)
  - Intensity-dependent pathway segregation (Liu 2020 Neuron):
    - Low (0.5mA): PROKR2 → vagal-adrenal (anti-inflammatory)
    - High (3.0mA): spinal sympathetic (different pathway, PROKR2-independent)
  - Point specificity molecular basis (PROKR2 distribution: 16.7% lumbar vs 6.1% thoracic DRG)
  - Timing matters: high-intensity EA CAN WORSEN inflammation if timed wrong (Ma lab)
  - 2025-2026 breakthroughs: spatial omics, flexible bioelectronics for De Qi, precision acupuncture/AI
- §5.3 Framework hypothesis: acupuncture = controlled multi-system activation at convergence zones 🔴

**§6 — Body System Pathways Activated**

**§7 — Comparison With Related Methods**
- 8+ variants table:
  - TCM body acupuncture (classical, 361 points)
  - Japanese (Kiiko/Toyohari/Meridian Therapy/Shonishin): thinner needles, shallower, palpation-based, blind practitioner tradition, Sugiyama guide tube
  - Korean (Sa-am 4-needle, Sasang constitutional, KHT 404 hand points, pharmacopuncture/bee venom)
  - French auricular (Nogier inverted fetus, 3 phases, VAS/RAC, 7 frequencies)
  - Battlefield (Niemtzow 2001, 5 ASP ear points, VA 2,800+ trained, $4.05/session vs $115-294/week opioids)
  - Western/medical (White 2009, neuroanatomical point selection, segmental, trigger point)
  - Scalp (Jiao cortical mapping, YNSA somatotopes, Zhu dynamic)
  - Laser (non-invasive, 632-980nm, emerging evidence)
  - Dry needling (scope-of-practice debate, 93% trigger point-acupoint overlap, same needles different theory)
  - EA vs manual (EA: 15/52 brain regions vs manual: 7/52)
- Key philosophical differences: TCM vs neuroanatomical vs constitutional vs microsystem

**§8 — Individual Variation & Contraindications**
- Responder vs non-responder: neuroimaging predictors (83% accuracy, gray matter volume), gut microbiota, cytokines
- Genetic variation: COMT, OPRM1 polymorphisms
- Contraindications: pacemakers (EA only), bleeding disorders, pregnancy (certain points), infection at site
- Safety: 86 documented deaths across entire literature (vs 5,000-16,500 NSAID deaths/year US)
- Pneumothorax (most common serious adverse event), infections (dramatically reduced by single-use needles)

**§10 — Open Questions**
- Q1: Can sham debate be resolved? (sham laser = most inert option?)
- Q2: Optimal dose-response for each condition? (no standardized guidelines exist)
- Q3: How to predict responders pre-treatment? (biomarkers emerging but not validated)
- Q4: Do different variants work through different mechanisms? (Japanese shallow vs Chinese deep)
- Q5: Long-term neuroplastic effects? (most evidence short-term)
- Q6: Can precision acupuncture (AI/omics-guided) outperform traditional pattern differentiation?
- Q7: TARA project outcomes? (Langevin/NCCIH)
- Q8: Will pharmacopuncture (bee venom, herbal injection) prove superior to plain needling?
