### Central Question
```
PRESSURE vs NEEDLE at the SAME POINT:
  → What mechanisms are SHARED?
  → What mechanisms are LOST (no tissue penetration)?
  → What mechanisms are UNIQUE to pressure (gate control, self-administration)?

Key answer from Track A:
  Acupuncture = 5/5 PNS mechanisms (A01 §4.2)
  Acupressure = 2/5 PNS mechanisms (gate control + somatovisceral reflex)
  + "some Aδ" with deep pressure
  BUT: different circuits, NOT weaker-version-of-same-thing

3 divergence points:
  1. Fiber recruitment: Aβ (gate-closing) vs Aδ/C (gate-opening → descending opioid)
  2. Depth/magnitude: surface pressure vs collagen-winding needle grasp
  3. Evidence base: nearly ALL landmark molecular studies used needle/EA
```

### Tier Decision
TIER 1 lite (2 sessions) justified because:
1. 642 RCTs = substantial evidence base requiring thorough clinical section
2. Central mechanism question (pressure vs needle) is nuanced
3. Auricular acupressure = functionally distinct sub-modality (ABVN → NTS)
4. Self-administration capability = unique practical dimension
5. Structural blinding problem = key methodological theme
6. BUT: much can cross-reference B01 (same points) + B03 (shared pressure)
