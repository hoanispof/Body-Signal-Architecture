# AP-09 Pericardium (PC) — Phase 2 Child Plan

## Meridian Overview

Hand Jueyin Pericardium (手厥阴心包经). 9 standard points (PC1-PC9).
Paired with Triple Burner (Hand Shaoyang TE).
Path: lateral chest → axilla → medial upper arm → forearm (between
palmaris longus + flexor carpi radialis) → palm → middle fingertip.
Peak time: 19:00-21:00. Element: Ministerial Fire (相火).

TCM role: "outer defense of the Heart" (心之外卫). Functions similar
to Heart channel but treats conditions where direct Heart needling
is avoided (classical prohibition against needling HT directly in
some traditions → Pericardium substitutes).

## Anatomical Themes to Track

1. **Median nerve gradient:** PC channel follows median nerve from
   proximal (chest/arm) through forearm to hand. Different branches
   accessed at different points. PC6/PC7/PC5/PC4 all between palmaris
   longus + FCR = same inter-tendon cleft, different depths/levels.

2. **Ghost Point pair (PC7 + PC8):** Both on Pericardium channel = unique.
   No other channel contributes 2 Ghost Points. Traditional significance
   for mental disorders. Modern question: what neural pathway underlies
   the "Ghost" indication pattern?

3. **Nerve transition:** PC1-PC2 = medial pectoral/musculocutaneous nerves.
   PC3-PC7 = median nerve territory. PC8-PC9 = median + ulnar palmar
   digital branches. Three distinct neural zones on one channel.

4. **Five Shu complete set:** PC channel has all 5 Five Shu points in
   Phase 2 (PC9 Well → PC8 Spring → PC7 Stream → PC5 River → PC3 Sea).
   First complete Five Shu set in Atlas = opportunity to analyze the
   classical system against modern anatomy.

## Special Considerations

- **Pneumothorax risk at PC1:** 4th intercostal space. Must note
  max safe depth and angle. Only PC point with thoracic risk.
- **Carpal tunnel at PC7:** At carpal tunnel entrance. Median nerve
  risk similar to PC6 but more superficial.
- **Bloodletting at PC3:** Classical technique (pricking to bleed) at
  cubital fossa. Near brachial artery = vascular risk. Traditional
  for acute heat conditions.
- **Cross-tradition:** Forearm PC points overlap with flexor trigger
  points (Dorsher). PC8 palm = interesting Marma correspondence
  (Talahridaya — heart of palm). PC1 may overlap with pectoral
  trigger points.
