## §2 — Five Structural Revisions

Phase 4 identified five structural revisions to the BSA framework.
Phase 5 implemented all five:

### Revision 1: Regional Range Model (replaces "60-70% segmental")

**Previous framework position (C04 S3.4):** Segmental convergence
covers "~60-70% of acupoint effects."

**Revised position:** Segmental contribution varies 0-100% by body
region. "60-70%" is the cross-body AVERAGE of a continuous range:
- ~100%: paravertebral / Back-Shu points
- 30-50%: mid-limb points
- 0%: distal limb (ST36, LI4, BL67)
- N/A: head/ear (cranial nerve architecture, not spinal)

**Files updated:** C04 S3.4 (rank 2 entry), C04 S3.5.6, A07 S8 Q1,
A09 S3.2

**Source:** ⚡ notes #8, #17, #21

---

### Revision 2: Dual-Axis Model (extends convergence zone model)

**Previous framework position (A09 S2.2):** Acupoints = multi-system
convergence zones. Convergence predicts clinical importance.

**Revised position:** Clinical importance = convergence breadth ×
mechanism specificity. Two orthogonal axes, both contribute independently:
- High on both (ST36, PC6) = strongest clinical points
- High specificity, low convergence (BL67) = still Cochrane-level
- Low on both (Jing-Well) = limited clinical utility

Convergence predicts mechanism CLARITY (how well we understand WHY)
but NOT clinical EFFICACY (WHETHER it works).

**Files updated:** A09 S2.2

**Source:** ⚡ notes #22, #54, #55

---

### Revision 3: 19 Mechanism Patterns (replaces implicit single-mechanism)

**Previous framework position:** BSA discusses mechanisms at system
level (Track A: A01-A06). Implicitly suggests one or few mechanisms.

**Revised position:** 19 distinct mechanism patterns across 6 taxonomy
levels (Local Tissue → Spinal Segmental → Supraspinal Brainstem →
Cranial Nerve Direct → Cortical → Hormonal/Neuroendocrine). No single
Track A system explains all acupoint effects. The "non-segmental gap"
is not one missing mechanism but reflects 10+ point-specific pathways.

**Files updated:** A09 S3.2 (synthesis), A07 S8 Q1, C04 S3.5.6

**Source:** ⚡ notes #1, #5, #7, #25, #29, #32 (and full Phase 4)

---

### Revision 4: Three Architectural Regions (adds cranial nerve class)

**Previous framework position:** BSA implicitly assumes spinal pathway
architecture. Core-Body-Architecture S2.2 discusses face/ear/foot but
as regional variations, not a distinct pathway class.

**Revised position:** ~33% of Atlas entries operate through cranial
nerve architecture with direct brainstem access:
- Pattern 1: V1+C2 dual cranial (GV20 → oxytocin)
- Pattern 2: Pure V1 (Yintang → trigeminal → brainstem)
- Pattern 3: ABVN+V3+C2-C3 triple + vagal (auricular points)

This is NOT a variation of spinal somatovisceral reflex but a separate
architectural class requiring no dorsal horn convergence.

**Files updated:** Core-Body-Architecture S2.2

**Source:** ⚡ notes #33, #35, #39

---

### Revision 5: Molecular Diversity (extends PROKR2 discovery)

**Previous framework position (A02):** PROKR2 = breakthrough molecular
mechanism for acupuncture (Liu 2021 Nature).

**Revised position:** PROKR2 is one of at least 6 identified molecular
effectors, each at a different point through a different circuit:
- ST36: PROKR2 (causal)
- GV20: oxytocin (causal)
- PC6: GABA
- LI11: p47phox
- MS6: 5-HT2AR/KCC2
- ST36 local: adenosine

PROKR2 is sufficient (proven at ST36) but NOT necessary (microsystems
work without it). 13 of 19 patterns lack molecular characterization.

**Files updated:** A09 S2.2

**Source:** ⚡ notes #2, #3, #34, #53

## §3 — Additional Updates

### 3.1 Modest Effect Size Nuance (A09 S3.3)

The Vickers 2012-2018 "relatively modest" real-sham difference
applies to the GENERAL chronic pain pool. PC6 for PONV specifically
is drug-equivalent (ondansetron-level, Cochrane: Lee & Fan 2009).
Point-specific efficacy can exceed the pooled average significantly.

### 3.2 ABVN Gradient (B02 S3.3, S5.2.1)

taVNS evidence applies to cymba conchae specifically (100% ABVN).
ABVN gradient across auricular zones: cymba 100% → cavum ~45% →
antihelix body 73% → tragus mixed → crura 9% → lobule/scaphoid 0%.
Transferring taVNS evidence to non-cymba points is NOT supported.

### 3.3 SP6 Modality Reversal (B02 S10 Q6)

SP6 acupressure > acupuncture for dysmenorrhea — one of 5
modality-effect relationships showing optimal modality is
point-specific, not universal.

### 3.4 BL67 Thermal Model Violation (B08 S5.3)

BL67 is the #1 unsolved question across 520 Atlas entries. Zero of
19 mechanism patterns explains BL67→breech. The Thermal-Point
Convergence Model's L5 prediction definitively fails here.

### 3.5 Spleen Viscerotome Extension (A07 S1.6)

Spleen viscerotome T6-T8 (standard, greater splanchnic) extended to
T6-T11 including lesser splanchnic nerve (T10-T11). BL20 Back-Shu
at T11-T12 corresponds to this extended range.

### 3.6 LI4 Pregnancy Contraindication (B01 S8.3)

LI4 pregnancy contraindication is PRECAUTIONARY, not mechanistic.
>6,700 women received acupuncture including LI4 during pregnancy
with no adverse obstetric events.

### 3.7 Per-Point Dose-Response (Core-Therapy S6.3)

Dose-response cannot be studied as a single curve per therapy — it
must be studied per-point-per-mechanism. Atlas Phase 4 provides
initial data: PC6 modality-independent, SP6 pressure > needle,
BL67 moxa-only, HT7 possibly A-beta, Wrist-Ankle Acupuncture/Korean
Hand Therapy subcutaneous-only.

### 3.8 Scalp Priming Principle (Core-Therapy S3.1.4)

Scalp acupuncture works as a neuroplasticity PRIMER, not standalone.
Scalp acupuncture + concurrent rehabilitation >> scalp acupuncture
alone (SUCRA gap: 97.9% vs
17.8% in aphasia). Added to body region hierarchy.

### 3.9 Hypothalamic-Pituitary Axis Gap (A06)

Atlas Phase 4 Pattern 17 (Hormonal/Neuroendocrine, Level 6) is
0% covered in current Track A files. A06 is flagged as natural
home for future expansion. Note added, not full section.

## §7 — Impact Assessment

### What the Atlas Changed About BSA

The Acupoint Atlas project (Phases 1-5) produced the following
measurable impacts on the BSA framework:

1. **Expanded A09 S2.2** from convergence-zone model to dual-axis
   model with molecular specificity dimension
2. **Replaced C04 S3.4** "60-70% segmental" with regional range model
3. **Added cranial nerve architecture** to Core-Body-Architecture
   as a distinct pathway class (~33% of entries)
4. **Updated 3 Open Questions** from "gap/unknown" to "partially
   resolved" (A09 S3.2, A07 S8 Q1, Core-Therapy S6.3)
5. **Added ABVN gradient mapping** to B02 (new quantitative data)
6. **Documented 6 molecular effectors** where previously only PROKR2
   was highlighted
7. **Added scalp priming principle** to Core-Therapy body region
   hierarchy (new therapeutic paradigm)

### What Remains Unsolved

1. **BL67 mechanism** — 4 ⚡ notes, #1 unsolved. No pathway identified.
2. **13 of 19 mechanism patterns** lack molecular characterization
3. **Hypothalamic-pituitary axis** not formally integrated into Track A
4. **Individual dose-response** still the SINGLE LARGEST gap (now
   reframed as per-point-per-mechanism problem)
