### 1.3 Key Findings from Survey

**Top 10 convergence points:**
1. BL10 = 8/8 (ONLY perfect score — suboccipital region)
2-10: ST25, BL40, GB20, GB30, CV4, CV12, CV17, EX-HN5, EX-B2 = 7/8

**Lowest convergence points (1/8, TCM only):**
- Jing-Well tips: LU11, ST45, PC9, GB42, GB44
- Mid-limb outlier: LI13
- Intraoral: EX-HN9, EX-HN10, EX-HN11, EX-HN12, EX-HN13
- Technique-specific: EX-HN6 (Erjian ear apex), EX-HN7 (Qiuhou retro-orbital)
- Upper extremity extra: EX-UE1-6, EX-UE10
- Lower extremity distal: EX-LE11, EX-LE12
- Back extra: EX-B7

**Major patterns identified:**
1. Proximal-distal gradient: trunk > proximal limb > distal limb > digits
2. GV spinal corridor: remarkably uniform 6/8 (GV1-GV16), sharp cranial drop
3. ST abdominal band: ST19-ST30 all ≥5/8, peaking at ST25 (7/8)
4. KI trunk uniformity: KI12-KI27 almost all 5/8
5. BL dominance: 36/67 points ≥5/8 (54%), BL10 = only 8/8
6. Jing-Well minimum: digit-tip points consistently lowest
7. TE/SI weakness: lowest mean convergence among regular meridians
8. BL67 paradox: 2/8 convergence but strongest single-indication evidence
