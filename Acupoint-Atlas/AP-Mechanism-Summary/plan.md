### 1.3 Key Themes from ⚡ Notes

**A. Mechanism diversity is the rule:**
No universal acupuncture mechanism exists. Each point accesses a
different pathway determined by local anatomy. The highest-evidence
points (ST36, PC6, LI4, SP6, BL67) all use DIFFERENT mechanisms.

**B. Segmental range: 0% to ~100%:**
C04's "60-70% segmental" is an average over a 0-100% range:
- 0%: BL67, ST36, LI4 (non-segmental dominant)
- ~30-40%: PC6 (partial segmental)
- ~50%: SP6 (balanced)
- ~100%: Back-Shu points (fully segmental)

**C. Cranial nerve architecture:**
Head/ear points operate through fundamentally different architecture:
V1, V3, ABVN, C2-C3 → direct brainstem access without spinal cord.
Three documented patterns: GV20 (V1+C2), Yintang (pure V1),
Auricular (triple: ABVN+V3+C2-C3).

**D. Point-specific molecular effectors:**
Two confirmed: ST36→PROKR2, GV20→oxytocin. Each uses different
molecules through different circuits. Molecular specificity beyond
pathway specificity.

**E. PROKR2 sufficient but NOT necessary:**
Wrist-Ankle Acupuncture, Abdominal, Korean Hand Therapy microsystems work subcutaneously without De Qi.
At least one PROKR2-independent therapeutic pathway exists.

**F. BL67 = #1 unsolved question:**
4 ⚡ notes. 0% segmental. Cochrane-level evidence. Zero mechanism.
No known pathway from S1 to uterine function.

**G. Dual-axis model (from Phase 3):**
Clinical importance = convergence breadth + mechanism specificity.
Two orthogonal axes — both contribute independently.

## 2. Document Structure — AP-Mechanism-Summary.md

### §1 — Overview & Scope
What this synthesis covers. Relationship to Phase 3 (convergence)
and Phase 5 (framework impact).

### §2 — Mechanism Taxonomy
Classification of all mechanism types found across 520 entries.
Hierarchy: local/peripheral → segmental → supraspinal → cranial.
Molecular vs pathway vs system-level mechanisms.

### §3 — The Mechanism Catalog
Full documentation of all 10+ distinct mechanism patterns.
Per-pattern: pathway, molecular basis, points using it, evidence level.
The "no universal mechanism" conclusion.

### §4 — Segmental vs Non-Segmental Distribution
The 0-100% range documented across Atlas entries.
Body region × segmental contribution.
Why C04's "60-70%" is an average not a rule.
Segmental model = valid for trunk/paravertebral, not for distal limb.

### §5 — Cranial Nerve Architecture
Head and ear points = different neural architecture.
Three cranial nerve access patterns (GV20, Yintang, Auricular).
ABVN gradient mapping across auricular zones.
Brainstem fingerprints by zone.

### §6 — Molecular Specificity
PROKR2 (ST36), Oxytocin (GV20), Adenosine (local), GABA (PC6).
Point-specific molecular effectors.
PROKR2 sufficient but NOT necessary (microsystem evidence).
fMRI cortical fingerprints (ST36, HT7, LR3 = distinct patterns).

Per-body-region gap density.
