# AP-04 Spleen (SP) — Phase 2 Child Plan

## Meridian Overview

Foot Taiyin Spleen (足太阴脾经). 21 standard points (SP1-SP21).
Paired with Stomach (Foot Yangming ST).
Path: medial big toe (SP1) → medial foot → anterior to medial
malleolus → medial leg (SP6 at 3 cun above malleolus, SP9 at
medial knee) → medial thigh → inguinal region (SP12) → lateral
abdomen → lateral chest → ends at axillary line 6th intercostal
space (SP21). Internal pathway: enters abdomen, connects to Spleen
and Stomach, ascends through diaphragm, spreads in tongue root.
Peak time: 09:00-11:00. Element: Earth (土).

TCM role: "Spleen governs transformation and transportation"
(脾主运化). Controls digestion, absorption, ascending of clear Qi,
holding Blood in vessels, governing flesh/muscles. Spleen Qi
Deficiency = most common digestive TCM pattern. Earth is the
"center" element — generates post-natal Qi from food. Paired
Interior-Exterior with Stomach (ST, 45 points).

## Phase 1 Entry

SP6 (Sanyinjiao, 三阴交) — Tier A, COMPLETE (2026-08-06).
Three Yin Crossing point. Not in any standard 8-category system
yet clinically one of the most important points in TCM. Key findings:
dual-pathway mechanism (segmental S2-S4 + non-segmental PAG),
PTNS-SP6 convergence (independent modern medical validation),
acupressure > acupuncture paradox for dysmenorrhea. Deep Front
Line fascial access. 3 ⚡ Framework notes.

## Anatomical Themes to Track

1. **Five Shu COMPLETE — fifth set:** SP1 Well/Wood → SP2 Spring/Fire
   → SP3 Stream/Earth → SP5 River/Metal → SP9 He-Sea/Water.
   SP = Earth organ, so horary at Stream (SP3) where Earth on Earth.
   CONFIRMS AP-01 prediction. Fifth Five Shu set in Atlas (after PC,
   HT, LR, LU). Five Element × Five Shu orthogonality advances to
   4/5 unique roles per column (with KI/Water needed for 5/5 full proof).

   Five Element role assignments for SP (Earth organ):
   - SP1 Well/Wood: Ke CONTROLLER (Wood controls Earth)
   - SP2 Spring/Fire: TONIFICATION (Fire generates Earth)
   - SP3 Stream/Earth: HORARY (Earth = Earth)
   - SP5 River/Metal: SEDATION (Earth generates Metal, child drains)
   - SP9 Sea/Water: Ke SUBJECT (Earth controls Water)

2. **Saphenous nerve dominance on medial leg:** A09 confirms SP channel
   follows femoral/saphenous nerve trajectories (Dorsher: odds against
   chance ~1 in 34 million). Primary neural pathway: saphenous nerve
   (L3-L4) = sensory branch of femoral nerve. SP4-SP9 all in saphenous
   territory. Compare: LR channel also on medial leg — LR uses
   saphenous for LR4-8 (confirmed in AP-12). Question: how do SP and
   LR points differentiate when sharing saphenous innervation? Answer
   likely involves subtle branch differences and anterior (SP) vs
   posterior (LR) tibial border positioning.

3. **Three-Yin convergence zone (SP-LR-KI):** SP6 = named crossing
   point. AP-12 child plan raised question: "how anatomically distinct
   are LR6/SP6/KI7 when all three share saphenous nerve territory?"
   SP8 and SP9 also in this convergence zone. Answer this question
   during Batch 2 and 4 with anatomical specifics.

4. **Deep Front Line (DFL) access:** SP6 at DFL anchor (tibialis
   posterior). SP channel tracks DFL-related musculature on medial leg.
   A09: "SFL ↔ Stomach/Spleen, DFL ↔ Kidney/Liver." SP crosses both
   SFL and DFL territory. SP6 Phase 1 entry established: DFL provides
   fascial continuity from medial leg to pelvic floor → may explain
   acupressure > acupuncture paradox.

5. **Neural zone transitions — 4+ zones:**
   - Foot (SP1-SP3): medial plantar / dorsal digital nerves (L4-S1)
   - Medial foot/ankle (SP4-SP5): saphenous nerve (L3-L4) + superficial
     peroneal contributions
   - Medial leg (SP6-SP9): saphenous nerve (L3-L4) + deep: posterior
     tibial (L4-S3) at SP6
   - Medial thigh (SP10-SP12): saphenous → femoral cutaneous, transition
     to femoral main trunk. Inguinal: ilioinguinal/iliohypogastric
   - Abdomen (SP13-SP16): intercostal nerves T8-T12
   - Chest (SP17-SP21): intercostal nerves T2-T5
   Potentially 6 neural zones — more than LR (4) and HT/PC (3 each).
   Widest segmental span in Atlas: L4-S3 (foot/leg) to T2 (chest) =
   at least 15 spinal segments.

6. **Trunk segment: abdominal + chest wall (SP12-SP21):** 10 points
   on trunk = nearly half of total points. Unusual for a Yin channel
   (compare: LU has only LU1-LU2 on trunk; HT has only HT1; LR has
   LR13-LR14). SP has the LONGEST trunk segment of any Yin channel.
   All trunk points are intercostal nerve territory. Pneumothorax risk
   at chest points (SP17-SP21). This is important because it means SP
   spans from toe to chest — the longest anatomical range of any Yin
   meridian actually represented by points.

7. **SP4 Eight Confluent + Chong Mai:** SP4 (Luo) + PC6 (Luo) = one
   of 4 Confluent pairs opening extraordinary vessels. Opens Chong Mai
   (Sea of Blood vessel = "Sea of the 12 meridians") + Yin Wei Mai.
   Chong Mai governs Blood and menstruation — connects to SP10
   (Xuehai, Sea of Blood) thematically. Clinical: SP4+PC6 for
   abdominal/chest pain, menstrual disorders, cardiac conditions.

8. **Great Luo of Spleen (SP21):** The Great Connecting Vessel of
   Spleen = unique category in entire meridian system. Traditional
   theory: all Luo vessels ultimately connect through the Great Luo.
   SP21 = the only point in this category. Deficiency pattern: whole
   body flaccid/weak. Excess: whole body aching. This is a theoretical
   construct — mechanism unclear. Located at 6th intercostal space on
   axillary line = near the end of the channel's surface trajectory.

9. **SP1-LR1 big toe complementarity:** SP1 (medial nail corner) and
   LR1 (lateral nail corner) = same toe, opposite sides. Both are
   Well/bloodletting points. SP1 = hemostasis (stops bleeding,
   especially uterine). LR1 = emergency consciousness revival
   (standard Well protocol). Two traditions, same anatomical structure,
   different clinical roles. Neural: SP1 = medial plantar / saphenous;
   LR1 = deep peroneal. Different innervation despite adjacent location.

10. **Impedance negative finding:** A09: Spleen meridian does NOT
    show lower impedance along its path (unlike PC and LI which do).
    This is a negative finding that limits one proposed mechanism
    (electrical conductance pathway) for the SP channel specifically.
