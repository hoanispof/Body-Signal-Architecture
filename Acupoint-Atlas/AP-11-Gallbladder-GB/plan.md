## 1. Meridian Profile

**Trajectory:** GB1 (outer canthus) → zigzag across temporal/parietal skull (GB2–GB12) → forehead/occiput (GB13–GB20) → shoulder (GB21) → lateral trunk (GB22–GB28) → lateral hip (GB29–GB30) → lateral thigh/knee (GB31–GB34) → lateral leg (GB35–GB39) → ankle/foot (GB40–GB44).

**Unique features:**
- **20 head points** (GB1–GB20) — most cranial points of ANY channel. By comparison, BL has ~10 (BL1–BL10), ST has ~8 (ST1–ST8).
- **Shaoyang pair with TE** — same-name pairing. TE23→GB1 facial transition COMPLETE (documented in AP-10).
- **Jueyin-Shaoyang paired channel with LR** — Yuan-Luo pairing: LR3(Yuan)+GB37(Luo), GB40(Yuan)+LR5(Luo).
- **Dai Mai (Belt Vessel) hub:** GB41 opens Dai Mai (Eight Confluent), GB26–GB28 are GB-Dai Mai meeting points.
- **Yang Wei Mai trajectory:** multiple head points on Yang Wei Mai path (GB13–GB20 region).
- **Migraine dominance:** 8/11 top migraine points are Shaoyang (GB+TE). GB20–GB8–TE5 core prescription: 90.48% confidence (established in AP-10).

**Element:** Wood (paired with LR). Same Sheng cycle as LR but Yang manifestation.

**Neural span (preliminary):**
- Head: CN V1 (supraorbital), CN V2/V3 (auriculotemporal, zygomaticotemporal), greater/lesser occipital (C2–C3)
- Neck/shoulder: CN XI (accessory), cervical plexus (C3–C4)
- Trunk: intercostal (T-segments), iliohypogastric/ilioinguinal (T12–L1)
- Hip: superior/inferior gluteal, sciatic territory
- Leg: lateral femoral cutaneous (L2–L3), common peroneal (L4–S2), superficial peroneal (L4–S1)
- Foot: superficial/deep peroneal, sural

**Estimated 8–10+ neural zones** — may rival or surpass TE's record (8 zones, including 3 cranial nerves).

## 3. Five Shu System

GB = Yang channel of Wood organ. Yang Five Shu: Well=Metal, Spring=Water, Stream=Wood, River=Fire, He-Sea=Earth.

| Five Shu | Element | Point | Five Element Role | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|-------------------|-------|
| Well | Metal | GB44 (Zuqiaoyin) | **Ke Controller** | Metal controls Wood |
| Spring | Water | GB43 (Xiaxi) | **Tonification** | Water generates Wood (Mother) |
| Stream | Wood | GB41 (Zulinqi) | **Horary** | Matches organ element. ALSO Eight Confluent (Dai Mai) |
| River | Fire | GB38 (Yangfu) | **Sedation** | Wood generates Fire (Son) |
| He-Sea | Earth | GB34 (Yanglingquan) | **Ke Subject** | Wood controls Earth. ALSO Hui-Meeting Tendons + Lower He-Sea + Ma Dan-yang |

**Paired channel comparison (LR, Yin Wood):**
| Role | LR (Yin) | GB (Yang) |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Horary | LR1 (Well/Wood) | GB41 (Stream/Wood) |
| Tonification | LR8 (He-Sea/Water) | GB43 (Spring/Water) |
| Sedation | LR2 (Spring/Fire) | GB38 (River/Fire) |
| Ke Controller | LR4 (River/Metal) | GB44 (Well/Metal) |
| Ke Subject | LR3 (Stream/Earth) | GB34 (He-Sea/Earth) |

Same 5 roles, different Five Shu positions — confirms Yin-Yang orthogonality (AP-01 Obs. 22). Same ELEMENTS map to same ROLES, but the Five Shu type that carries each element reverses between Yin and Yang channels.
