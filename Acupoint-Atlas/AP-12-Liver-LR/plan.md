# AP-12 Liver (LR) — Phase 2 Child Plan

## Meridian Overview

Foot Jueyin Liver (足厥阴肝经). 14 standard points (LR1-LR14).
Paired with Gallbladder (Foot Shaoyang GB).
Path: lateral nail corner of big toe (LR1) → dorsum of foot →
medial leg (along tibia, posterior to SP channel) → medial knee
(LR8) → medial thigh (adductor group) → inguinal region →
lateral/lower abdomen → subcostal area (LR13, LR14).
Internal pathway: ascends through diaphragm, spreads in ribs,
follows throat, connects to eye system, reaches vertex (→ GV20).
Peak time: 01:00-03:00. Element: Wood (木).

TCM role: "Liver governs the free flow of Qi" (肝主疏泄). Controls
emotional regulation, tendon/sinew function, stores Blood, opens
to the eyes. Liver Qi Stagnation = most commonly diagnosed TCM
pattern in modern clinical practice. Longest Yin channel by path
length (toe to subcostal) though only 14 points. Paired Interior-
Exterior with Gallbladder (GB, 44 points).

## Anatomical Themes to Track

1. **Four neural zones:** LR channel traverses 4 distinct neural
   territories — more than any completed meridian:
   - Foot (LR1-4): deep peroneal nerve, L4-L5
   - Medial leg (LR5-7): saphenous nerve (branch of femoral), L3-L4
   - Knee/thigh (LR8-12): saphenous → obturator nerve, L2-L4
   - Trunk (LR13-14): intercostal nerves, T6-T8
   Compare: HT = 3 zones (brachial plexus → ulnar forearm → digital),
   PC = 3 zones (pectoral → median forearm → palmar digital).

2. **Dual Front-Mu points:** LR13 (Front-Mu of Spleen) and LR14
   (Front-Mu of Liver) = UNIQUE in all 14 meridians. No other channel
   contains two Front-Mu points. Both are subcostal/lateral abdomen.
   LR13 is additionally Hui-Meeting of Zang organs (1 of only 8
   Influential points in the body).

3. **Medial leg convergence zone:** LR5-8 travel the medial leg where
   three Yin foot channels converge: Liver (most posterior), Spleen
   (middle), Kidney (most posterior on leg but crosses at ankle). SP6
   (Sanyinjiao, "Three Yin Intersection") is the named crossing point.
   Question: how anatomically distinct are LR6/SP6/KI7 when all three
   share the saphenous nerve territory (L3-L4)?

4. **Five Shu complete set — third in Atlas:** LR1 Well/Wood → LR2
   Spring/Fire → LR3 Stream/Earth → LR4 River/Metal → LR8 Sea/Water.
   UNIQUE among completed Five Shu: Liver is the WOOD organ, so LR1
   (Jing-Well/Wood) = horary point = same element as organ. This means
   the horary point is at the extreme distal (toe tip), unlike HT
   (HT8 Spring/Fire at palm) and PC (PC9 Well/Wood also at fingertip
   but Wood ≠ Fire, so it's tonification, not horary).

5. **Five Element complete system at Liver:** All Five Element
   relationships mappable:
   - LR8 (Water) = tonification (mother nourishes Wood in Sheng)
   - LR2 (Fire) = sedation (child drains Wood in Sheng)
   - LR1 (Wood) = horary (same element as organ)
   - LR4 (Metal) = control/restraint (Metal controls Wood in Ke)
   - LR3 (Earth) = Yuan-Source (neutral — access to Original Qi)
   Compare with Heart's Fire triad (HT3/HT7/HT8). Liver has a
   FOUR-element functional system plus Yuan access — most complete
   Five Element logic of any channel so far.

6. **Inguinal danger zone (LR9-12):** Four points traverse the
   femoral triangle area. Femoral artery, femoral vein, femoral nerve.
   LR10 = classical needling prohibition in some texts. LR12 = directly
   lateral to femoral artery pulse. Highest concentration of vascular
   risk points on any single channel.

7. **Liver-Gallbladder Interior-Exterior pair:** LR (Foot Jueyin) ↔
   GB (Foot Shaoyang). Host-Guest technique: Liver as host → LR3
   (Yuan) + GB37 (Luo, Guangming). GB as host → GB40 (Yuan, Qiuxu) +
   LR5 (Luo, Ligou). Unique: LR13 (Zhangmen) is Front-Mu of SPLEEN,
   not Liver — the Liver channel "hosts" the Spleen's Front-Mu point.

8. **Genitourinary theme across channel:** Multiple LR points share
   genital/urinary indications: LR1 (hernia, uterine bleeding), LR4
   (urinary retention), LR5 (genital itching, hernia), LR8 (genital
   conditions), LR9 (urinary retention), LR12 (genital pain). This is
   consistent with the channel pathway curving around external genitalia.
   Compare with KI channel (also genitourinary) — different mechanism?

## Special Considerations

- **Pneumothorax risk at LR14:** 6th intercostal space. Must note max
  safe depth and angle. LR13 at free end of 11th rib = lower risk but
  still requires caution (splenic/hepatic injury with deep insertion).
- **Femoral vessels at LR10-12:** LR10 classically prohibited from
  needling. LR12 directly lateral to femoral artery pulse. Highest-risk
  zone on the channel.
- **Medial knee at LR8:** Near medial inferior genicular artery and
  saphenous nerve. Risk = moderate (standard knee point protocols).
- **Cross-tradition LR-GB comparison:** Interior-Exterior pair. LR =
  medial (Yin), GB = lateral (Yang). Same organ system, opposite
  pathways. GB34 (Yanglingquan) = Hui-Meeting of Tendons — Liver
  "governs tendons" → anatomical logic of the LR-GB pairing.
- **Three-Yin intersection question:** LR, SP, KI channels converge
  at medial leg. SP6 = intersection. How do LR5/LR6 differentiate
  from SP6/SP9 and KI7/KI8 in the same anatomical zone? Answer may
  involve subtle nerve branch differences or empirical tradition.
