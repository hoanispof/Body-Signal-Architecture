# AP-05 Heart (HT) — Phase 2 Child Plan

## Meridian Overview

Hand Shaoyin Heart (手少阴心经). 9 standard points (HT1-HT9).
Paired with Small Intestine (Hand Taiyang SI).
Path: heart (internal) → axilla (HT1) → posteromedial upper arm →
medial forearm (ulnar side, along flexor carpi ulnaris) → wrist
crease (HT7) → palm → radial nail corner of little finger (HT9).
Peak time: 11:00-13:00. Element: Fire (火).

TCM role: "Heart houses the Shen" (心藏神). Governs consciousness,
cognition, emotional regulation, sleep. Shortest regular meridian
(9 points). Classical prohibition in some traditions against direct
Heart needling → Pericardium substitutes — but Heart channel points
are widely used in modern practice.

## Anatomical Themes to Track

1. **Ulnar nerve gradient:** HT channel follows the ulnar nerve from
   proximal arm through forearm to hand. Neural zones: HT1 (brachial
   plexus, mixed C5-T1), HT2 (ulnar nerve proximal, near brachial
   artery), HT3 (ulnar nerve at cubital tunnel), HT4-HT7 (ulnar
   nerve forearm, all along FCU tendon), HT8-HT9 (ulnar + digital
   nerves in hand). Compare with PC median nerve gradient.

2. **Heart-PC parallel:** Two "cardiac" channels, same 9-point count,
   adjacent wrist anatomy (HT = ulnar side, PC = median/radial side).
   HT7 uses A-beta/ulnar, PC6 uses A-delta/median — DIFFERENT
   pathways for overlapping clinical effects. Compare Five Shu
   assignments and mechanism patterns.

3. **Forearm FCU quartet:** HT4-HT5-HT6-HT7 all on the radial border
   of the flexor carpi ulnaris tendon, spaced 0.5-1.5 cun apart.
   Same anatomical question as PC inter-tendon quartet (PC4-7): is
   there real anatomical distinction between adjacent points on the
   same tendon border?

4. **Five Shu complete set:** Second complete Five Shu in Atlas (after
   PC). HT9 Well/Wood → HT8 Spring/Fire → HT7 Stream/Earth →
   HT4 River/Metal → HT3 Sea/Water. Heart = Fire organ:
   - HT9 (Wood) = tonification point (mother of Fire)
   - HT7 (Earth) = sedation point (child of Fire)
   - HT8 (Fire) = horary point (same element as channel)
   Element relationships map to clinical logic: sedate excess (HT7
   calms), tonify deficiency (HT9 strengthens), clear same-element
   pathology (HT8 clears Heart Fire directly).

5. **Mental disorder spectrum:** Heart channel = primary Shen channel.
   Multiple points share "calming mind" function but with different
   mechanisms: HT7 (A-beta → limbic), HT3 (Water sedation → Heart
   Fire), HT8 (Fire clearing), HT5 (Luo connection → speech center).
   Document mechanism attribution for each.

## Special Considerations

- **Axillary risk at HT1:** Brachial plexus + axillary artery.
  Needle medial to artery pulse. Avoid medial-directed insertion
  (lung puncture risk). Highest-risk point on the channel.
- **Cubital tunnel at HT3:** Ulnar nerve passes through cubital
  tunnel at medial elbow. Careful needling required. Risk of
  electric shock sensation to digits 4-5.
- **FCU tendon quartet (HT4-7):** All four points on the same
  tendon border. Spacing 0.5-1.5 cun. Same anatomical question
  as PC inter-tendon quartet — distinction may be empirical.
- **Cross-tradition HT-PC comparison:** Both channels serve cardiac
  function. Document where cross-tradition mappings overlap vs
  diverge. Dorsher: FCU trigger points map to HT channel, palmaris
  longus/FCR trigger points map to PC channel.
