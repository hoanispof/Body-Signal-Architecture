# AP-16 Auricular Microsystem — Child Plan

## 1. Scope

**File:** AP-16-Auricular.md (hiện có 734 dòng, 3 entries Phase 1)
**Standard:** Chinese Standard GB/T 13734-2008 (revised from 1992)
**Total points:** 91 named auricular points across 9 anatomical zones
**Phase 1 done:** 3 entries (Ear Shen Men TF4, Sympathetic AH6a, Point Zero)
**Phase 2 remaining:** 88 entries

**Why auricular microsystem is different from meridian-based entries:**
- Somatotopic organization (inverted fetus model), NOT meridian/channel logic
- ABVN vagal innervation = PRIMARY mechanistic data (not segmental convergence)
- Most evidence is PROTOCOL-level (NADA 5-point, BFA 5-point, multi-point combinations), NOT individual-point-level
- Organ-specific mapping: 🟡/🔴 (much weaker than body acupoint evidence)
- Template: microsystem template (plan.md §3.2), NOT meridian template

**Key methodological decisions:**
1. Use Chinese standard zone codes (TF4, AH6a, CO1, etc.) as primary identifiers
2. Cross-reference: WHO MA- prefix, Nogier/European names, BFA/NADA names where applicable
3. ABVN innervation status = mandatory field for every entry (determines vagal access)
4. Evidence: taVNS literature as primary modern evidence source for concha zone points
5. Somatotopic correspondence: record Nogier model mapping AND known neural pathway

## 2. Auricular Anatomy — ABVN Innervation Map

Critical context for tier assignment. ABVN density determines vagal access potential.

| Zone | ABVN Innervation | Primary Nerve | Vagal Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cymba conchae | 100% (Peuker & Filler 2002) | ABVN dominant | HIGHEST |
| Antihelix | 73% | ABVN + V3 | HIGH |
| Cavum conchae | 45% | ABVN + V3 + C2-C3 | MODERATE |
| Tragus | 45% | ABVN + V3 | MODERATE |
| Triangular fossa | Variable | V3 primary, ABVN variable | LOW-MODERATE |
| Scaphoid fossa | 0% | great auricular nerve C2-C3 (100%), NO V3 — corrected per ⚡8 | NONE |
| Helix | Low | V3 + C2-C3 | LOW |
| Antitragus | Minor/variable | C2-C3 great auricular nerve primary, V3 not primary — corrected per ⚡7 | MINIMAL |
| Lobule | 0% | Great auricular C2-C3 | NONE |

**Implication:** Concha zone points (CO1-CO18) have strongest vagal rationale. Lobule points (LO1-LO9) have NO vagal access — effects must operate through cervical plexus / trigeminal pathways only.

## 7. Special Considerations

### 7.1 taVNS Evidence Transfer
taVNS targets cymba conchae (100% ABVN) specifically. Evidence from taVNS studies (Frangos 2015 fMRI, JAMA RCT 2024 insomnia, depression SR/MA) applies to cymba conchae zone points (upper CO points) but CANNOT be transferred to non-concha zones. Each entry must clarify whether taVNS evidence is directly applicable or only tangentially relevant.

### 7.2 BFA Point Mapping
BFA uses Niemtzow's brain-structure names (fMRI-based rationale), not Chinese standard codes.
Points #1-3 are on the MEDIAL (inner) surface of the ear — distinct from external Chinese standard points:
- BFA #1 "Cingulate Gyrus" — antihelix wall, inner/medial surface. Named after fMRI pain activation pattern. NOT identical to AT1 Subcortex (which is external antitragus).
- BFA #2 "Thalamus" — inner wall of antitragus, directly opposite external AT4 (Temple/Sun). Central pain processing target.
- BFA #3 "Omega 2" — internal/medial surface of helix. Due to thinness of auricle, exterior stimulation contacts this point. Used for headache, musculoskeletal pain.
- BFA #4 "Point Zero" = helix root area (done, Phase 1)
- BFA #5 "Shen Men" = TF4 (done, Phase 1)
ASP gold semi-permanent needles (<2 mm), retained 3-5 days. Sequential bilateral alternation with walk test.
Cross-reference BFA names within relevant zone entries. BFA medial-surface points may need separate subsection within zones since they don't correspond 1:1 to external Chinese standard points.

### 7.3 NADA Point Mapping
NADA 5-point protocol (Michael O. Smith, Lincoln Hospital, 1974-1985). Bilateral filiform needles, 30-45 min group setting.
- NADA #1 "Shen Men" = TF4 (done, Phase 1)
- NADA #2 "Sympathetic" = AH6a (done, Phase 1)
- NADA #3 "Kidney" = CO10 — cymba conchae (100% ABVN territory), willpower, fear resolution
- NADA #4 "Liver" = CO12 — cymba conchae (100% ABVN), anger/impulsivity modulation
- NADA #5 "Lung" = CO14 — cavum conchae (45% ABVN), grief, emotional release. The original single-point treatment.
Evidence: Bullock 1989 Lancet positive (n=80), Avants 2000 JAMA IM positive (n=82 cocaine), but Bullock 2002 largest RCT (n=503) NEGATIVE. Cochrane 2006 cocaine: "no evidence." Overall: mixed/inconclusive.
Cross-reference NADA designation in these CO entries (Batch 1-2).

### 7.4 Nogier vs Chinese Differences
The Nogier (European) and Chinese systems assign DIFFERENT organ mappings to some ear zones. Key disagreements:
- Liver/Gallbladder placement in concha
- Kidney location (superior vs inferior concha)
- Some antitragus point functions
Document both systems where they diverge; note that WFAS standards primarily follow Chinese 2008 convention.

### 7.5 Cross-Reference with Body Acupoints
Several auricular points share names with body acupoints:
- Ear Shen Men (TF4) ↔ HT7 Shénmén (done in Phase 1)
- Ear Taiyang (AT4) ↔ EX-HN5 Taiyang
- Ear Ear Apex (HX5) ↔ EX-HN6 Erjian
Document these parallels in cross-system mapping.

### 7.6 Heading Structure in AP-16 File
Phase 1 used ## for each major point. Phase 2 will use:
- ## Zone Name (e.g., ## Concha — Organ Points)
- ### Point Code — Name (e.g., ### CO1 — Mouth)
This groups points by zone while maintaining navigability.
