## Architectural Questions to Monitor

1. **YANG FIRE vs YIN FIRE Five Shu:** SI and HT share Fire element but differ in Five Shu element sequence. Both have Tonification at Wood, Sedation at Earth, Horary at Fire — but at DIFFERENT Shu positions. Does Shu position affect clinical application, or only element role?

2. **SCAPULAR ZIGZAG anatomy:** SI9–SI15 = 7 points on scapula. What nerve(s) supply this region? Suprascapular nerve (C5-C6) for supraspinous/infraspinous; axillary nerve (C5-C6) for deltoid area; dorsal scapular nerve (C5) for rhomboids. How many zones?

3. **HT-SI INTERIOR-EXTERIOR completion:** First fully documented interior-exterior pair. Heart Fire → SI Heat transfer (TCM): mechanism? HT Luo (HT5) → SI pathway (TCM describes this, no anatomy). What patterns emerge from having both channels complete?

4. **EIGHT CONFLUENT at SI3 → Du Mai:** SI3 (Hand Taiyang) + BL62 (Foot Taiyang) opens Du Mai (spine midline). Both Taiyang points. Anatomical basis? Neural pathway from ulnar hand → spinal cord → brainstem?

5. **TAIYANG FAMILY pattern:** SI (Hand) + BL (Foot). Both run posterior. SI crosses scapula; BL has Back-Shu points. Family-level patterns? Are Taiyang points more posterior-body concentrated than Yangming (LI+ST)?

6. **WINDOW OF HEAVEN at SI16 (+SI17?):** SI16 Tianchuang = definite Window of Heaven (Ling Shu Ch.2). SI17 Tianrong = Window of Heaven in some texts but debated. Does SI have 1 or 2 Window of Heaven points? How do they fit the cervical ring?

7. **AUDITORY TERMINUS (SI19):** "Palace of Hearing." Three channels converge at pre-auricular area (SI19, TE21, GB2). Neural basis? Auriculotemporal nerve (V3) → trigeminal pathway? Compare with LI20 (trigeminal endpoint).

8. **ULNAR NERVE CORRIDOR → SCAPULAR TRANSITION:** SI1–SI8 on ulnar nerve territory. SI9+ on scapular nerves. Where is the exact boundary? How many neural zones total on SI channel?

9. **SI1 LACTATION mechanism:** Classical lactation point at little finger. T1 dermatome → somato-visceral reflex to T4 mammary innervation? Same logic as ST18/CV17? Or different pathway?

10. **SI11 TIANZONG as scapular anchor:** Infraspinous fossa center = infraspinatus trigger point. Clinical evidence? SI11's role in scapular point cluster — is it the "SI equivalent of LI11" (functional anchor with most clinical use)?
