## 1. Meridian Profile

**Trajectory:** ST1 (infraorbital, below eye) → cheek/jaw (ST2–ST7) → temporal (ST8) → neck (ST9–ST12) → anterior chest (ST13–ST18) → anterior abdomen (ST19–ST30) → anterior thigh (ST31–ST33) → knee (ST34–ST36) → anterolateral leg (ST37–ST41) → dorsal foot (ST42–ST45).

**Unique features:**
- **LONGEST Yang meridian** — 45 points spanning face to toe. Widest anatomical coverage of any single channel.
- **Yangming pair with LI** — only Six-Channel pair that meets at the face. LI20 (nasolabial fold) → ST1 (infraorbital). The Yangming face transition = ONLY inter-channel connection at the anterior face.
- **Paired Interior-Exterior with SP** — Earth Yin-Yang pair. SP trajectory mirrors ST on medial vs anterolateral leg. SP-ST combined = 66 points covering the Earth element.
- **THREE Lower He-Sea points hosted:** ST36 (own, Stomach), ST37 (Large Intestine), ST39 (Small Intestine). No other channel hosts Lower He-Sea for 3 organs. ST channel as "collection channel" for Yang Fu Qi at the leg.
- **Phase 1 molecular anchors:** ST36 (PROKR2→vagal-adrenal, strongest mechanism in acupuncture) and ST25 (segmental sympathetic, pathway segregation). Same meridian, opposite pathways.
- **TWO Ghost Points:** ST6 (#7 鬼床) — if confirmed across sources. One of only a few channels with Ghost Point representation.
- **Window of Heaven at ST9** — carotid artery access. Safety-critical.
- **12 abdominal points (ST19–ST30)** — most abdominal points of any channel. Direct segmental access to gastric (T6–T9) and intestinal (T10–L1) viscerotomes.

**Element:** Earth (土). Stomach = Yang Earth organ (Fu). Peak time: 07:00–09:00.

**TCM role:** "Stomach governs reception and rotting/ripening" (胃主受纳腐熟). Downward-bearing Qi. Paired with Spleen's ascending/transforming function. Together = post-natal Qi production. "Sea of Water and Grain" (水谷之海). Stomach Qi's presence in the pulse = fundamental vitality marker ("有胃气则生，无胃气则死").

## 3. Five Shu System

ST = Yang channel of Earth organ. Yang Five Shu: Well=Metal, Spring=Water, Stream=Wood, River=Fire, He-Sea=Earth.

| Five Shu | Element | Point | Five Element Role | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|-------------------|-------|
| Well | Metal | ST45 (Lidui) | **Sedation** | Earth generates Metal (child drains parent) |
| Spring | Water | ST44 (Neiting) | **Ke Subject** | Earth controls Water (土克水). Ma Dan-yang Star |
| Stream | Wood | ST43 (Xiangu) | **Ke Controller** | Wood controls Earth (木克土) |
| River | Fire | ST41 (Jiexi) | **Tonification** | Fire generates Earth (mother nourishes child) |
| He-Sea | Earth | ST36 (Zusanli) | **Horary** | Earth = channel element. DONE (Phase 1). Also: Command Point, Ma Dan-yang, Lower He-Sea of ST |

### Paired Channel Comparison (SP Yin Earth ↔ ST Yang Earth)

| Five Element Role | SP (Yin) — Five Shu Type | ST (Yang) — Five Shu Type | Same Element? |
|-------------------|--------------------------|---------------------------|---------------|
| Horary (Earth) | SP3 (Stream/Earth) | ST36 (He-Sea/Earth) | ✓ |
| Tonification (Fire) | SP2 (Spring/Fire) | ST41 (River/Fire) | ✓ |
| Sedation (Metal) | SP5 (River/Metal) | ST45 (Well/Metal) | ✓ |
| Ke Controller (Wood) | SP1 (Well/Wood) | ST43 (Stream/Wood) | ✓ |
| Ke Subject (Water) | SP9 (He-Sea/Water) | ST44 (Spring/Water) | ✓ |

**5/5 elements match roles.** Same elements map to same Five Element roles, but DIFFERENT Five Shu positions between Yin and Yang. Confirms Yin-Yang orthogonality (AP-01 Obs. 22). This is the FIRST paired-channel comparison within the SAME element (Earth), and the SEVENTH documented Five Shu set in the Atlas (after PC, HT, LR, LU, SP, GB).

### Same-Name Comparison (LI Hand Yangming ↔ ST Foot Yangming)

| Five Shu | Element | LI Point | ST Point | Same Role? |
|----------|---------|----------|----------|------------|
| Well | Metal | LI1 (Horary) | ST45 (Sedation) | ✗ |
| Spring | Water | LI2 (Sedation) | ST44 (Ke Subject) | ✗ |
| Stream | Wood | LI3 (Ke Subject) | ST43 (Ke Controller) | ✗ |
| River | Fire | LI5 (Ke Controller) | ST41 (Tonification) | ✗ |
| He-Sea | Earth | LI11 (Tonification) | ST36 (Horary) | ✗ |

**0/5 roles match at same Five Shu position.** Same-name channels (Yangming) have the same element sequence (Metal→Water→Wood→Fire→Earth at Well→Spring→Stream→River→Sea) but DIFFERENT Five Element roles because the organ elements differ (LI=Metal organ, ST=Earth organ). The role is determined by the relationship between the Shu-element and the organ-element, not by the channel family.

## 6. Architectural Questions to Monitor

1. **YANGMING FACE TRANSITION (LI20→ST1):** What happens anatomically? LI20 at nasolabial fold (CN V2 infraorbital) → ST1 below eye (CN V2 infraorbital + CN V1 supraorbital junction). Same nerve territory → explains classical same-name connection? LI channel crosses midline here (左之右右之左). Does ST also cross?

2. **LOWER HE-SEA COLLECTION:** ST hosts 3 Lower He-Sea points for 3 Fu organs (ST, LI, SI). BL hosts 2 (BL, TE). GB hosts 1 (GB). Pattern: Yangming leg = primary collector, Taiyang leg = secondary, Shaoyang leg = self-only. Why? Is there an anatomical basis (nerve convergence at anterior leg)?

3. **GHOST POINT #7 (ST6):** Where does ST6 fit in the 13 Ghost Point architecture? Ghost Points documented so far: LU11(#2), SP1(#3), PC7(#4), PC8(#9), LI11(#12). ST6(#7) = 6th documented. Masseter/jaw location — classical association with jaw clenching in mania/epilepsy?

4. **WINDOW OF HEAVEN (ST9):** 10th Window of Heaven point documented. ST9 = carotid bifurcation access. Cervical ring architecture: how does ST9 relate to LI18 (nearby, same SCM region but different approach)?

5. **ST12 CROSSING HUB:** Supraclavicular fossa = where ALL Yang channels cross. How many channels actually converge here? What's the anatomical basis? Brachial plexus + subclavian vessels at same site.

6. **ABDOMINAL GRADIENT (ST19→ST30):** 12 abdominal points at 2 cun lateral to midline, every 1 cun from 6 cun above to 5 cun below umbilicus. Do segmental levels (T7→L1) match gastric/intestinal viscerotome progression? Compare with ST25 (T10, confirmed segmental for LI).

7. **SP-ST PAIRED CHANNEL ANATOMY:** SP runs medial leg, ST runs anterolateral. Both end at the foot. How do their neural zones compare? SP has 6 zones — will ST match or exceed?

8. **YANG XI-CLEFT FUNCTIONAL SPLIT:** ST34 (Xi-Cleft) — does it follow the Yang pattern (pain/inflammation) established at LI7? Or does it have blood-specific functions (Yin pattern)?

9. **ST17 PROHIBITION:** How to handle a non-therapeutic landmark point in the Atlas? Precedent: no previous channel has had a point that is purely a locating landmark.

10. **ST38 CONTRALATERAL SHOULDER:** Empirical technique with no clear mechanism. Tibialis anterior (same muscle as ST36) but no known supraspinal pathway to shoulder. Placebo? Fascial? Worth noting as clinical practice without mechanism.

11. **ST40 PHLEGM MECHANISM:** TCM's primary phlegm-resolving point. Any modern evidence? Is "phlegm" mappable to any Track A system? Possible: mucus secretion (respiratory), metabolic syndrome markers, lipid metabolism?

12. **ST42 DORSALIS PEDIS PULSE:** Yuan-Source at pulse-palpation site, parallels LU9 (Yuan at radial pulse) and LR3 (Yuan near dorsalis pedis). Classical pulse diagnosis at Yuan-Source points — systematic pattern?

13. **SEVEN FIVE SHU SETS:** ST completes the 7th set (after PC, HT, LR, LU, SP, GB). Remaining: LI (DONE but not counted as separate set? — check), SI, BL, KI, TE. Actually LI Five Shu IS documented in AP-02. TE documented in AP-10. So this should be 7th or later — verify running count.

14. **NEURAL ZONE COUNT:** ST spans CN V1 → CN V2 → CN V3 → cervical plexus (C2–C4) → intercostal (T1–T12) → femoral/lateral cutaneous (L2–L4) → peroneal (L4–S1) → digital (L5–S1). Potentially 8–10 zones. Compare with GB (17 zones, current record) and TE (8 zones).
