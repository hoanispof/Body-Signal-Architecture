## 1. Meridian Profile

**Trajectory:** KI1 (foot sole) → KI2–KI6 (medial ankle, below/around medial malleolus) → KI7–KI8 (2 cun above medial malleolus) → KI9 (medial calf, 5 cun above malleolus) → KI10 (medial popliteal fossa) → KI11–KI21 (abdomen, 0.5 cun lateral to midline, pubic symphysis to 6 cun above umbilicus) → KI22–KI27 (chest, 2 cun lateral to midline, 5th ICS to infraclavicular).

**Unique features:**
- **DENSEST special-category cluster** — KI1–KI10 = ALL 10 points have special designations (5 Five Shu + Yuan-Source + Luo-Connecting + Xi-Cleft + Eight Confluent + 2 extraordinary vessel Xi-Clefts). No other meridian has 10 consecutive points ALL bearing category designations.
- **TWO extraordinary vessel Xi-Clefts hosted** — KI8 (Xi-Cleft of Yin Qiao Mai) and KI9 (Xi-Cleft of Yin Wei Mai). Most meridians host 0–1 extraordinary vessel Xi-Cleft; KI hosts 2.
- **ELEVEN Chong Mai meeting points** — KI11–KI21 ALL meet with Chong Mai (冲脉, "Sea of Blood"). No other regular meridian has 11 consecutive points sharing one extraordinary vessel. KI's abdominal trajectory essentially IS the Chong Mai's abdominal course.
- **Yuan-Source = Five Shu overlap (KI3)** — Confirms the Yin pattern: Yuan-Source point = Stream point (same location). Already confirmed at LU9, HT7, PC7, SP3, LR3. KI3 = 6th/6th Yin confirmation (100% consistent).
- **Innermost abdominal line** — KI runs at 0.5 cun lateral to midline in abdomen (CV=0, KI=0.5, ST=2, SP=4 cun). "Four-line abdominal tiling" (ST plan Obs. 17).
- **Lateral shift at chest** — Abdominal points at 0.5 cun lateral; chest points shift to 2 cun lateral. Trajectory discontinuity at diaphragm level.
- **Paired with BL (Foot Taiyang)** — Interior-Exterior Water pair. BL (67 points) not yet done; KI data will be complete before BL.
- **Same-name pair HT (Hand Shaoyin)** — COMPLETE. Five Shu comparison available.

**Element:** Water (水). Kidney = Yin Water organ (Zang). Peak time: 17:00–19:00.

**TCM role:** "Kidney stores Essence (精), governs growth/development/reproduction" (腎藏精，主生長發育生殖). Kidney = root of congenital Qi (先天之本). Controls water metabolism, receives Qi from Lung (納氣). "Opens into the ears" (開竅於耳), governs bones/marrow. TCM Kidney = functional system encompassing adrenal function, reproductive endocrinology, bone/marrow health, and water metabolism — NOT identical to the anatomical kidney (B01 §1.3.5).

KI = Yin channel of Water organ. Yin Five Shu: Well=Wood, Spring=Fire, Stream=Earth, River=Metal, He-Sea=Water.

| Five Shu | Element | Point | Five Element Role | Notes |

BL (Yang) Five Shu: Well=Metal, Spring=Water, Stream=Wood, River=Fire, He-Sea=Earth.

### Same-Name Comparison (HT Hand Shaoyin ↔ KI Foot Shaoyin)

HT (Fire organ, Yin channel). HT Five Shu roles:
- Tonification: HT9 (Well/Wood) — Wood generates Fire
- Horary: HT8 (Spring/Fire) — Fire = channel element
- Sedation: HT7 (Stream/Earth) — Fire generates Earth
- Ke Subject: HT4 (River/Metal) — Fire controls Metal? No — Metal controls Fire? Wait.

Actually: Ke Controller = element that CONTROLS the organ element. For HT (Fire): Water controls Fire → He-Sea (Water) = Ke Controller. Ke Subject = element that organ element CONTROLS. Fire controls Metal → River (Metal) = Ke Subject.

| Five Shu | Element | HT Role | KI Role | Same Role? |
|----------|---------|---------|---------|------------|
| Well | Wood | Tonification | Sedation | ✗ |
| Spring | Fire | Horary | Ke Subject | ✗ |
| Stream | Earth | Sedation | Ke Controller | ✗ |
| River | Metal | Ke Subject | Tonification | ✗ |
| He-Sea | Water | Ke Controller | Horary | ✗ |

**0/5 roles match at same Five Shu position.** Same-name channels (Shaoyin) have the same element sequence (Wood→Fire→Earth→Metal→Water at Well→Spring→Stream→River→Sea) but DIFFERENT Five Element roles because the organ elements differ (HT=Fire, KI=Water). The role is determined by the relationship between the Shu-element and the organ-element, not by the channel family. Confirms same-name divergence pattern (also 0/5 at ST-LI Yangming).

## 6. Architectural Questions to Monitor

1. **YUAN-SOURCE = STREAM OVERLAP (KI3):** 6th/6th Yin channel confirmation. With 6/6 Yang confirmed as Yuan ≠ Five Shu, the full 12/12 pattern is: Yin Yuan = Stream (always same point), Yang Yuan = separate (always different point). Is there an anatomical basis? Yin Yuan = at the wrist/ankle where Stream points naturally fall; Yang Yuan = slightly distal, at a separate landmark.

2. **DENSEST CATEGORY CLUSTER (KI1–KI10):** ALL 10 points have special designations. Why? Is this an artifact of the meridian's trajectory (short ankle-to-knee segment packs many functions) or a reflection of the ankle's anatomical richness (posterior tibial nerve, multiple fascial planes)?

3. **TWO EXTRAORDINARY VESSEL XI-CLEFTS (KI8, KI9):** KI hosts Xi-Clefts for both Yin Qiao Mai and Yin Wei Mai. Pattern: Yin extraordinary vessel Xi-Clefts cluster on Yin channels (KI8, KI9, SP13 for Yin Wei?). Yang extraordinary vessel Xi-Clefts cluster on Yang channels (BL59 Yang Qiao, GB35 Yang Wei). Verify this pattern.

4. **CHONG MAI CORRIDOR (KI11–KI21):** 11 consecutive points ALL meeting with Chong Mai. Does this mean the Chong Mai's abdominal trajectory IS the KI trajectory at 0.5 cun? If so, are KI abdominal points better understood as "Chong Mai points hosted on the KI channel" (analogous to Lower He-Sea points hosted on ST for other organs)?

5. **LATERAL SHIFT AT CHEST:** KI runs at 0.5 cun lateral in abdomen but 2 cun lateral in chest. This is the ONLY meridian with such a trajectory discontinuity. Anatomical basis: sternum width? Rib cage vs rectus abdominis?

6. **THREE "SPIRIT" NAMES (KI23 神封, KI24 灵墟, KI25 神藏):** Three consecutive chest points with Spirit/Spirit/Spirit-related names. Does this reflect a classical association between Kidney Qi in the chest and the spirit? Compare with HT (Spirit gate = HT7 Shenmen).

7. **SEDATION POINT = MOST STUDIED POINT (KI1):** The Five Element sedation point of KI channel is its Tier A emergency/arousal point. Sedation should "drain excess." Does intensity-dependent arousal at KI1 function as "draining excess from the head" (vertex headache, hypertension) — i.e., the traditional sedation concept captures a real descending effect?

8. **POSTERIOR TIBIAL ARTERY ACCESS (KI3):** KI3 = palpable pulse at posterior tibial artery. Parallels LU9 (radial artery) and ST42 (dorsalis pedis). Yuan-Source at pulse site = systematic pattern? With KI3, we have 3 Yuan-Source points at major pulse sites.

9. **KI3 TAIXI SCOPE:** KI3 is the single most prescribed point for "Kidney tonification" — the broadest-scope traditional indication. Does its evidence base justify this? Or is it another case where traditional prominence exceeds modern evidence (like ST45 insomnia)?

10. **MEDIAL LEG THREE-CHANNEL OVERLAP:** KI, LR, and SP all traverse the medial leg. SP6 crosses all three. How do nearby KI points (KI7, KI8) differentiate from LR5/LR6 and SP6/SP7 in the same anatomical zone? Already raised in LR plan §8.

11. **HOST-GUEST COMPLETION (KI↔BL):** KI3(Yuan)+BL58(Luo) vs BL64(Yuan)+KI4(Luo) = 6th Interior-Exterior Host-Guest pair. With BL not yet done, document from KI side and mark for BL cross-ref.

12. **LU7+KI6 EIGHT CONFLUENT PAIR:** The MOST cross-referenced extraordinary vessel pair in the Atlas (mentioned in AP-01, AP-06, AP-10). KI6 entry must consolidate all existing references.
