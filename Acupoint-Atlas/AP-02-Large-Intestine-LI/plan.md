## Architectural Questions to Monitor

1. **YANG vs YIN Five Shu:** How does Yang element assignment differ functionally? In Yin channels, tonification is always the Mother element. Same pattern in Yang?
2. **RADIAL NERVE gradient:** LI4 uses superficial radial nerve (C6-C7). How does innervation change proximally? Does LI parallel LU's radial artery corridor with a radial NERVE corridor?
3. **MIDLINE CROSSING:** LI channel crosses to contralateral face at LI20. What anatomical basis? Motor innervation of facial muscles is bilateral above the mouth.
4. **LI-LU PAIRING patterns:** LI6 (Luo) connects to LU. LI4 (Yuan) pairs with LU7 (Luo) in Host-Guest. Any deeper anatomical correspondence?
5. **HAND THREE LI (LI10) vs FOOT THREE LI (ST36):** Same naming pattern, same Yang Ming family. Functional parallel or divergence?
6. **NECK TRANSITION (LI17-LI18):** Channel crosses from musculocutaneous territory to deep cervical. Safety and neural zone implications.
7. **LI4→LI11 vascular axis:** Kuo 2015 showed needling LI4 increases blood flow at LI11. A second data point for distant intra-channel hemodynamic effects (after SP9→splenic flow).
8. **GHOST POINT LI11:** 12th of Sun Simiao's 13 Ghost Points (鬼臣). How does it fit the Ghost Point architectural pattern established in PC7/PC8?
9. **五里 NAMING CONVENTION:** LI13 (手五里) parallels LR10 (足五里). Both near major vessels, both classically prohibited. Systematic safety coding.
10. **SP10+LI11 DERMATOLOGY PAIR:** Already referenced in SP10 entry. LI11 is the Yang counterpart. IL-33/ST2 pathway data exists for this combination.
