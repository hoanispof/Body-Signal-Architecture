## 1. Meridian Profile

**Trajectory:** BL1 (inner canthus) → BL2 (medial eyebrow) → BL3–BL8 (scalp, 1.5 cun lateral to GV midline) → BL9 (occiput) → BL10 (posterior neck) → BL11–BL30 (medial back line, 1.5 cun lateral to spine, T1→S4) → BL31–BL34 (sacral foramina) → BL35 (coccyx) → BL36–BL40 (posterior gluteal/thigh/popliteal) → BL41–BL54 (outer back line, 3 cun lateral to spine, T2→S4) → BL55–BL67 (posterior calf/ankle/foot, ending at lateral 5th toenail).

**Unique features:**
- **LONGEST MERIDIAN, MOST POINTS.** 67 standard points — 22 more than ST (45), 23 more than GB (44). Covers the entire posterior body from eye to toe.
- **TWO PARALLEL BACK LINES (unique).** ONLY meridian in the 12-channel cycle with a bifurcated trajectory. Medial line (1.5 cun lateral, BL11–BL30) = organ Back-Shu points. Outer line (3 cun lateral, BL41–BL54) = "spirit residence" points. Both lines traverse the same spinal segments but at different lateral distances.
- **ALL 12 BACK-SHU POINTS hosted.** BL13/Lung, BL14/PC, BL15/Heart, BL18/Liver, BL19/GB, BL20/Spleen, BL21/Stomach, BL22/Triple Burner, BL23/Kidney, BL25/LI, BL27/SI, BL28/BL. No other meridian hosts more than 0 organ Back-Shu. 5/12 done in Phase 1 (BL13, BL15, BL18, BL20, BL23 = 5 Zang organs). 7 remaining (BL14, BL19, BL21, BL22, BL25, BL27, BL28 = PC + 5 Fu + BL itself).
- **TWO HUI-MEETING POINTS on medial line.** BL11 (Hui-Meeting of Bones) + BL17 (Hui-Meeting of Blood). Only channel hosting 2 of the 8 Hui-Meeting.
- **FOUR SACRAL FORAMINA points (BL31–BL34 "Baliao").** Direct access to sacral nerve roots S1–S4 via posterior sacral foramina. Most direct neural access to pelvic viscera in the Atlas.
- **BL40 = Four Command Points** for lumbar/back ("腰背委中求"). Also He-Sea of BL + Lower He-Sea of Bladder organ.
- **BL39 = Lower He-Sea of Triple Burner.** With BL39+BL40 documented, all 6 Lower He-Sea will be complete (ST36/ST37/ST39/GB34 already done).
- **BL62 = Eight Confluent (Yang Qiao Mai).** Paired with SI3 for Du Mai access. LAST remaining Eight Confluent point (7/8 done).
- **BL59 = Xi-Cleft of Yang Qiao Mai.** LAST remaining extraordinary vessel Xi-Cleft (3/4 done: KI8/Yin Qiao, KI9/Yin Wei, GB35/Yang Wei).
- **BL58 = Luo-Connecting.** 12th/12th regular Luo — LAST one in the Atlas.
- **BL64 = Yuan-Source.** 12th/12th regular Yuan-Source — LAST one in the Atlas.
- **BL63 = Xi-Cleft of BL.** 12th/12th regular Xi-Cleft — LAST one in the Atlas.
- **FIVE-SPIRIT NAMING on outer line.** BL42 魄户 (Po/Lung), BL44 神堂 (Shen/Heart), BL47 魂门 (Hun/Liver), BL49 意舍 (Yi/Spleen), BL52 志室 (Zhi/Kidney). The five psychospiritual aspects (五神) encoded in acupoint names.
- **BL1 = MOST multi-channel meeting point.** Meeting of BL, ST, SI, GB, GV, and Yin Qiao Mai (6 channels). No other point in the Atlas has this many channel convergences.
- **Paired with KI (Foot Shaoyin) — JUST COMPLETED.** Interior-Exterior Water pair. KI plan predicted BL Five Shu roles; can now verify. Host-Guest pairing: KI3(Yuan)+BL58(Luo) vs BL64(Yuan)+KI4(Luo).

**Element:** Water (水). Bladder = Yang Water organ (Fu). Peak time: 15:00–17:00.

**TCM role:** "Bladder governs Qi transformation (膀胱者，州都之官，津液藏焉，氣化則能出矣)" (Su Wen Ch.8). Primary function: water metabolism, urine storage/excretion. BL channel = much broader than organ: entire posterior body defense (Taiyang = "most Yang" = body's exterior shield), organ access via Back-Shu, and the spine/nervous system corridor. BL channel function >> BL organ function.

## 6. Architectural Questions to Monitor

1. **TWO PARALLEL BACK LINES.** ONLY meridian with bifurcated trajectory. Medial (1.5 cun) = organ Back-Shu. Outer (3 cun) = "spirit residence" points. Same spinal segments, different lateral distance. TCM: medial treats physical organ, outer treats organ's psychospiritual aspect. BSA question: does the 1.5 cun difference access different neural structures? Medial = dorsal ramus medial branch (facet joints, multifidus). Outer = dorsal ramus lateral branch (longissimus, iliocostalis). Different fiber composition → different reflex targets?

2. **DORSHER NON-MATCHING TRIAD (BL21, BL25, BL27).** 3/12 Back-Shu points do NOT match organ viscerotome (Dorsher 2009). BL21/Stomach: T12 vs T6-T10. BL25/LI: L4 vs T10-L2. BL27/SI: S1 vs T9-T11. Pattern: all 3 are Fu (Yang) organ Shu, and all 3 are BELOW their expected viscerotome. The 9 matching Shu are predominantly Zang (Yin) or at correct level. Does the Fu non-match reflect: (a) a systematic offset in classical assignment, (b) different innervation pathways for Fu organs, or (c) a non-segmental mechanism at these 3 points?

3. **BL40 AS FOUR COMMAND POINTS — NON-SEGMENTAL.** BL40 (popliteal, S1-S2 dermatome) treats lumbar/back pain (T12-L5). This is explicitly non-segmental (4-8 spinal segments distant). Mechanism: supraspinal? PAG descending modulation (like LI4)? Or tibial nerve → spinal cord convergence at lumbar enlargement? Important comparison with LI4 (also distal, also treats head — 10+ segments distant).

4. **BL1 MULTI-CHANNEL CONVERGENCE.** 6 channels at one point — unprecedented in Atlas. Anatomically: inner canthus = angular artery/vein + ophthalmic V1 + infraorbital V2 + angular branch of facial VII. Does multi-channel designation map to multi-nerve convergence? Or does it reflect the eye's importance in TCM (all channels contribute to vision)?

5. **FIVE-SPIRIT NAMING SYSTEM.** BL42/魄, BL44/神, BL47/魂, BL49/意, BL52/志 — each names a specific psychospiritual aspect (五神) of the corresponding Zang organ. This is the only acupoint naming system in TCM that explicitly encodes the five-spirit theory. From BSA perspective: can the outer BL line's psychological indications be explained by spinal-level access to autonomic pathways that mediate emotional states?

6. **SACRAL FORAMINA DIRECT ACCESS (BL31–34).** Unlike Back-Shu points (which access dorsal rami → somatovisceral reflex), the Baliao points enter the SACRAL FORAMINA → sacral nerve roots → pelvic viscera. This is the MOST direct neural access to pelvic organs in the entire Atlas. Equivalent to modern sacral nerve stimulation (SNS) for overactive bladder/incontinence. Is BL32 = the traditional precursor to modern SNS?

7. **T8 GAP.** No BL point at T8 level (BL17=T7, BL18=T9). T8 is the only thoracic level without a Back-Shu point. Diaphragm (T7) = anatomical boundary between thoracic and abdominal organs. Does the T8 gap reflect an actual gap in visceral innervation density at this transitional level?

8. **BACK-SHU COMPLETION INVENTORY.** With BL28, all 12 organ Back-Shu will be documented. Can then construct the full Back-Shu table: T3→S2, all 12 organs, segmental matching analysis. Dorsher's 9/12 matching can be verified entry-by-entry.

   More inventory completions than any other single meridian.
