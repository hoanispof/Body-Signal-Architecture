# AP-01 Lung (LU) — Phase 2 Child Plan

## Meridian Overview

Hand Taiyin Lung (手太阴肺经). 11 standard points (LU1-LU11).
Paired with Large Intestine (Hand Yangming LI).
Path: lateral chest below clavicle (LU1) → anterior axillary fold
→ medial upper arm (bicipital groove) → anterior forearm (radial
side, lateral to biceps tendon at elbow, then between
brachioradialis and flexor carpi radialis) → wrist (radial artery)
→ thenar eminence → radial nail corner of thumb (LU11).
Peak time: 03:00-05:00 (FIRST in Chinese clock cycle).
Element: Metal (金).

TCM role: "Lung governs Qi and respiration" (肺主气司呼吸), controls
the skin and body hair (皮毛), opens to the nose, dominates
diffusion and descent of Qi (宣发肃降). Paired Interior-Exterior
with Large Intestine (LI, 20 points). Metal emotion: grief (悲).
Metal season: autumn.

First meridian in the Qi circulation cycle — Ling Shu states Qi
begins its daily circulation at LU, flowing LU → LI → ST → SP →
HT → SI → BL → KI → PC → TE → GB → LR → back to LU.

## Anatomical Themes to Track

1. **TRIPLE designation at LU9:** Yuan-Source + Shu-Stream/Earth +
   Hui-Meeting of Vessels = 3 categories at one point. Most multi-
   category point in the entire Atlas. Compare LR13 (Front-Mu +
   Hui-Meeting = 2). Does triple designation reflect anatomical
   convergence at radial artery pulse?

2. **Tonification-Yuan overlap:** LU9 is simultaneously the
   tonification point (Earth = mother of Metal) AND the Yuan-Source
   point. In Liver, these roles are separate (LR8 tonification ≠
   LR3 Yuan). Systematic pattern: Yuan-Source always at Shu-Stream
   (Earth for Yin channels), so overlap occurs when mother element
   = Earth — specifically for Metal organs.
   
   Pattern across completed channels:
   - Liver (Wood): Yuan (LR3 Earth) ≠ tonification (LR8 Water). No overlap.
   - Heart (Fire): Yuan (HT7 Earth) = sedation (child of Fire). Overlap!
   - PC (Fire): Yuan (PC7 Earth) = sedation. Same as Heart.
   - Lung (Metal): Yuan (LU9 Earth) = tonification (mother of Metal). Overlap!
   
   Rule: Yuan always Earth → if Earth is mother, Yuan = tonification.
   If Earth is child, Yuan = sedation. Only Wood organ has no overlap.

3. **Five Shu complete set — fourth in Atlas:** LU11 Well/Wood →
   LU10 Spring/Fire → LU9 Stream/Earth → LU8 River/Metal → LU5
   Sea/Water. Lung is the Metal organ, so horary (self-element) falls
   at LU8 (River position) — different from Liver (Well), Heart
   (Spring), PC (Well). Each organ places the horary at a different
   Five Shu position.

4. **Radial artery corridor:** LU7, LU8, LU9 all located along or
   near the radial artery. LU9 = pulse diagnosis landmark (cun kou).
   LU8 = moxa prohibited (radial artery proximity). Three consecutive
   points sharing one named artery is unique in completed channels.
   Compare HT3-HT7 ulnar nerve corridor, PC4-PC7 median nerve corridor.

5. **Front-Mu of Lung on own channel:** LU1 is Front-Mu of Lung,
   located ON the Lung channel. One of only 3 organs with Front-Mu
   on own channel: Lung (LU1), Liver (LR14), Gallbladder (GB24).
   Most Front-Mu are on CV (6 organs) or hosted by other channels
   (ST25 = LI Front-Mu, LR13 = SP Front-Mu, GB25 = KI Front-Mu).
   
   Shu-Mu pairing: BL13 (T3) + LU1. Segmental question: LU1 at
   1st intercostal space ≈ T1 dermatome. BL13 at T3. Offset = 2
   segments. Compare LR: BL18 (T9) + LR14 (T6) = 3 segment offset.

6. **LU-LI Interior-Exterior pair:** Hand Taiyin ↔ Hand Yangming.
   Host-Guest: LU as host → LU9 (Yuan) + LI6 (Luo). Reverse: LI as
   host → LI4 (Yuan) + LU7 (Luo). Both Yuan points now in Atlas:
   LI4 (Phase 1 Tier A) and LU9 (Phase 2 Tier A). Can compare
   paired channel anatomy — LU runs anteromedial forearm, LI runs
   posterolateral forearm, converging at hand (1st web space vs thumb).

7. **Neural zones — likely 2-3:** Fewer transitions than LR (4):
   - Chest (LU1-LU2): lateral pectoral (C5-C7) + supraclavicular (C3-C4)
   - Arm-forearm (LU3-LU7): musculocutaneous → lateral cutaneous
     of forearm (C5-C6) + superficial radial nerve (C6-C7)
   - Wrist-hand (LU8-LU11): superficial radial + median digital (C6-C8)
   Zone count lower than LR because the arm-forearm segment is
   neurally more homogeneous. Compare HT/PC (3 zones each).

8. **Pneumothorax risk zone:** LU1 and LU2 are both chest points
   with pneumothorax risk. LU1 is the clinically more important one.
   Max safe depth must be documented. Compare LR14 (6th intercostal,
   also pneumothorax risk).

9. **Moxa prohibition at LU8:** Classical texts prohibit moxa at LU8
   due to radial artery proximity. This is a SPECIFIC TECHNIQUE
   prohibition (not all stimulation), unlike LR10 which has a
   NEEDLING prohibition. Different mechanisms: LU8 = thermal injury
   to artery, LR10 = mechanical puncture risk.

10. **"First meridian" in Qi cycle:** Lung = #1 in the 12-meridian
    circulation (Ling Shu). Qi begins at LU (03:00-05:00). Peak asthma
    episodes occur in early morning (03:00-05:00) — coincidence with
    LU peak time + cortisol nadir is a known observation, not a
    validated mechanism.
