# AP-18 Other Microsystems — Child Plan

## 1. Scope

**File:** AP-18-Other-Microsystems.md (new file)
**Total entries:** 9 (1 zone header + 4 Tier B overviews + 3 Tier C overviews + 1 cross-system comparison)
**Phase 1 done:** 0 entries
**Phase 2 remaining:** 9 entries

**Why AP-18 is different from AP-16 (Auricular) and AP-17 (Scalp):**
- AP-16 and AP-17 each cover ONE microsystem with a single national/WHO standard
- AP-18 covers MULTIPLE independent microsystems, each with its own paradigm
- Evidence is SYSTEM-LEVEL (protocol-based), not per-point — no microsystem in AP-18 has per-point clinical trials
- No WHO standardization for ANY system covered here
- All entries are SYSTEM OVERVIEWS (like YNSA/Zhu in AP-17), not individual point entries
- Point/zone inventories are EMBEDDED TABLES within each overview, not separate entries

**Key methodological decisions:**
1. System overview format for all entries — 345 Korean Hand Therapy points or 300+ Dien Chan points cannot have individual entries
2. Evidence assessment at SYSTEM level, not per-point
3. Cross-system comparison mandatory — these microsystems share a foundational claim (body → microzone projection) that must be evaluated consistently
4. Mechanism analysis: which systems have plausible neural pathways (like auricular ABVN) vs which rely on unvalidated projection theories
5. Template: microsystem template (plan.md §3.2), adapted for system-level overview
6. Innervation for each microsystem's anatomical territory = mandatory field

### 2.2 Microsystems Excluded (with rationale)

| System | Why excluded |
|---|---|
| Nose acupuncture (鼻针 Bí Zhēn) | Has Chinese national standard GB/T 21709.17-2009 but as a somatotopic microsystem (treating distant conditions via nose points), essentially zero evidence. Intranasal acupuncture for rhinitis has 2 SRs (2024, 14-21 RCTs) but these are local treatment, not microsystem application. No identified founder. Mentioned in zone header. |
| Tongue acupuncture (舌针 Shé Zhēn) | Covered in scalp acupuncture combination protocols (Feng 2022 SUCRA). Not an independent microsystem with its own point map. |
| Master Tung acupuncture (董氏针灸) | Not a microsystem — uses unique body-wide points (740+) that overlap/supplement standard 361. Better classified as an alternative point selection system, not a somatotopic microsystem. |
| Foot reflexology (zone therapy) | Covered in B06 reflexology analysis within BSA. Not acupuncture-based. Zone model (Ingham/Fitzgerald) does not map to acupoints. |

### 2.3 Key Observation: The 1970s Chinese Microsystem Wave

Five of the seven systems (Wrist-Ankle Acupuncture, Eye, ECIWO, Abdominal, and scalp acupuncture systems Jiao/Fang/Zhu covered in AP-17) emerged in China during the 1970s. This concentration likely reflects:
- Cultural Revolution-era emphasis on "worker-physician" pragmatism and acupuncture promotion
- Political mandate to develop simple techniques usable by barefoot doctors (赤脚医生)
- Same institutional environment (military hospitals, TCM universities) producing multiple innovations simultaneously
- Post-Mao 1980s: Korean (Korean Hand Therapy, Su Jok) and Vietnamese (Dien Chan) systems emerge in geographically adjacent cultures with strong acupuncture traditions

This is historically significant but does NOT validate or invalidate the systems — it explains the clustering.

## 3. Tier Assignment

### Tier B — System Overview (4 systems)

| System | Tier B Justification |

| Wrist-Ankle Acupuncture | 7+ SRs/MAs (Zhu 2014 33 RCTs n=723; cancer pain 2021 13 RCTs n=1,005; orthopedic postop 2022 12 studies n=845; acute ortho 2023 10 RCTs n=725; chronic pain 2023 19 RCTs n=1,380; lumbar sprain 2020 protocol; post-stroke SHS 2026). Unique subcutaneous technique with no De Qi. 12-point system = smallest, most standardized of AP-18 microsystems. |
| Korean Hand Therapy | 4+ PONV RCTs at K-D2 (PMID 12351304, 16288616, 24427460). Largest hand microsystem (345 points, 14 micro-meridians). Somatotopic model with hand innervation substrate (median C6-C8, ulnar C8-T1, radial C6-C7). Preliminary neuroimaging (TCD, fMRI). |
| Abdominal | 3 completed MAs (stroke motor 2021 21 RCTs n=1,473 PMID 34650503; stroke depression 2021 10 RCTs n=708; cervical spondylosis 2011 8 RCTs n=909) + 3 protocols + 2 methodological reviews. 107+ RCTs across 38 disease categories. ~35 total points (standard CV/KI/SP/ST + AB1-AB19 extra). "Sacred Turtle" (神龟图) somatotopy. Three depth layers (天人地). Sham-controlled RCT positive (Hong Kong 2017 n=154). |
| Eye | 3 SRs/MAs (stroke 2015 PMID 26161127 16 RCTs n=1,120; insomnia 2025 PMC 11977738 13 RCTs n=856; insomnia NMA 2025 PMC 12714621 31 RCTs n=2,293) + 1 scoping review (pain 2021 PMC 7989101 81 studies n=7,113) + 2 bibliometrics (stroke 195 studies n=15,466; pain 109 studies). Chinese national standard GB/T 21709.15-2009. Based on classical "Five Wheels and Eight Contours" (五轮八廓). 13 periorbital points in 8 areas / 4 regions. V1 trigeminal + orbicularis oculi innervation. |

### Tier C — Brief Overview (3 systems)

| System | Tier C Justification |
|---|---|
| ECIWO | No rigorous SR. 54 Chinese-language papers (1974-2016). Classified as pseudoscience by Chinese scientific establishment 1996 — founder's position revoked, research defunded. Theoretical importance (holographic biological principle) despite controversy. 6 acupoints on 2nd metacarpal; extensible to all long bones. Claims "1,000,000+ cases" without controlled evidence. |
| Su Jok | 2-3 narrative SRs (postoperative pain 2025, pain 2021 Indonesian). No meta-analysis. Multiple RCTs exist (post-cesarean n=120, lumbar surgery n=60, migraine n=47) but low quality. 8-10 ClinicalTrials.gov entries (2024-2026, primarily Turkey). National recognition in Russia, Kazakhstan, Serbia, Romania. Multi-layered correspondence (standard, insect, mini systems). |
| Dien Chan | Zero peer-reviewed evidence. Zero PubMed-indexed publications. 257 BQC points, 25+ overlapping reflex diagrams. Adopted by Cuban public health system (1980s). Discovery March 26, 1980 at Binh Trieu Drug Detoxification Center, HCMC. Unique Vietnamese origin. Clinical claims without controlled validation. |

## 4. Template Adaptation for AP-18

Each entry follows a modified microsystem overview template:

```markdown
### [System Name] — [Chinese/Korean/Vietnamese Name] ([Evidence Tier])

**Lịch sử và phát triển:**
  Founder, year, institutional context
  Development timeline and current status
  National/international recognition

**Mô hình somatotopic:**
  Somatotopic projection theory
  Point/zone inventory table (embedded)
  Anatomical landmarks

**Giải phẫu vùng microsystem:**
  Nerve supply of the microsystem territory
  Pathway to CNS
  Comparison with auricular (ABVN) and scalp (trigeminal/cervical) pathways

**Cross-system mapping:**
  Relationship to body acupoints
  Relationship to other microsystems (AP-16, AP-17)
  Points of convergence and divergence

**Bằng chứng hiện đại:**
  SRs/MAs (if any)
  Key RCTs
  Confidence tier per indication
  Gap analysis

**Cơ chế giải thích:**
  Proposed mechanism(s)
  Neural pathway plausibility
  Comparison with validated microsystem mechanisms (ABVN, trigeminovascular)
  ⚡ Framework note (if applicable)

**Đánh giá tổng thể:**
  Evidence strength relative to AP-16/AP-17
  Research gaps
  Clinical adoption status
```

### Batch 2: Korean Hand Therapy + Abdominal (2 entries)
**Korean Hand Therapy:** 345 points, 14 micro-meridians. Somatotopic model (middle finger = spine). K-D2 PONV evidence. Hand innervation substrate. Relationship to existing hand acupoints (LI4, PC8, HT8). No WHO standard.
**Abdominal:** Uses existing body acupoints with unique theory. "Turtle" (神龟图) somatotopic diagram. Superficial needling (0.2-0.8 cun). CV8 = navel center. Unique depth-function theory: 天 (superficial) → 人 (medium) → 地 (deep). SR for stroke motor recovery. Relationship to Front-Mu system.

### Batch 3: Eye + ECIWO + Su Jok + Dien Chan + Cross-System Comparison (5 entries)
**Eye:** 8 areas / 13 points around orbit. Five Wheels + Eight Contours. V1 trigeminal pathway. 2 SRs. Five Zang correspondence.
**ECIWO:** 2nd metacarpal, holographic principle. Theoretical significance vs evidence gap.
**Su Jok:** Derivative of Korean Hand Therapy concept. Hand = "Su," Foot = "Jok." Correspondence mapping.
**Dien Chan:** Vietnamese facial reflexology. 300+ points. Multiple correspondence maps. Cuban adoption.
**Cross-System Comparison:** Table comparing all 7 AP-18 systems + AP-16 + AP-17 across: paradigm, point count, evidence level, mechanism plausibility, standardization, clinical adoption.
