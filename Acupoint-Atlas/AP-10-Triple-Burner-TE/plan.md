# AP-10 Triple Burner (TE) — Phase 2 Child Plan

## Meridian Profile

- **Channel:** Hand Shaoyang (手少阳三焦经)
- **Total points:** 23 (TE1–TE23)
- **Phase 1 entries:** None — all 23 points are new
- **Paired channel (Interior-Exterior):** PC (Hand Jueyin) — **COMPLETE** (AP-09, 9/9)
- **Same-name Yang pairing:** GB (Foot Shaoyang) — not started

**Trajectory:** Ring fingertip (TE1) → dorsal hand → wrist → dorsal forearm → posterior elbow (olecranon) → posterior upper arm → shoulder → neck → behind ear → temporal region → lateral eyebrow (TE23).

**Element:** Fire (Minister Fire, 相火) — same element as SI (Sovereign Fire) and PC (Minister Fire). Second Fire-element Yang channel after SI.

**Peak time:** 21:00–23:00 (Hai shi).

**TCM role:** San Jiao (三焦) = Triple Burner/Triple Energizer. The most conceptually unique organ — described as "有名无形" (has name but no form). Controls water passages (决渎之官, 气化出焉). Distributes Yuan Qi (原气) from Kidney/Ming Men to all organs via Yuan-Source points. Governs Qi transformation across:
- Upper Jiao (上焦): mist (如雾) — Heart/Lung, respiration + distribution
- Middle Jiao (中焦): foam (如沤) — Spleen/Stomach, transformation
- Lower Jiao (下焦): ditch (如渎) — Kidney/Bladder, drainage

No direct Western anatomical correlate. BSA analysis suggests fascial compartment system + lymphatic drainage as closest modern mapping (A09 §2.2).

**Key channel feature:** ONLY channel to extensively serve the ear region (TE17-TE22 = 6 periauricular points). The three pre-auricular auditory points (SI19 + TE21 + GB2) represent convergence of three Yang channels at the ear.

## Five Shu System (Yang — Second Fire Channel)

| Shu Type | Point | Element | Five Element Role |
|----------|-------|---------|-------------------|
| Jing-Well | TE1 | Metal | Ke Subject (Fire克Metal, 火克金) |
| Ying-Spring | TE2 | Water | Ke Controller (Water克Fire, 水克火) |
| Shu-Stream | TE3 | Wood | Tonification (mother: 木生火) |
| Jing-River | TE6 | Fire | Horary (same element as channel) |
| He-Sea | TE10 | Earth | Sedation (child: 火生土) |

**Cross-channel comparison:** Identical element sequence to SI (also Fire). Yang Five Shu: Metal→Water→Wood→Fire→Earth. Both SI and TE assign the same Five Element roles at the same Five Shu positions — verification that the system is algebraically determined by channel element, not historically arbitrary.

**PC-TE Interior-Exterior Five Shu comparison:**
- PC (Yin Fire): Well/Wood → Spring/Fire → Stream/Earth → River/Metal → Sea/Water
- TE (Yang Fire): Well/Metal → Spring/Water → Stream/Wood → River/Fire → Sea/Earth
- Pattern: Yin and Yang Five Shu sequences are REVERSED (Wood↔Metal, Fire↔Water, Earth↔Earth — no, they use different element sequences entirely). The Horary point is the ONLY shared element position: PC5 River/Metal ≠ TE6 River/Fire. Actually PC has Horary at Spring/Fire (PC8), TE has Horary at River/Fire. Different Five Shu types, same element assignment for Horary.

## Anatomical Themes to Track

1. **PC-TE Interior-Exterior pair:** PC = median nerve (palmar/volar), TE = radial nerve (dorsal). Same forearm, opposite sides. Compare: neural zones, clinical breadth, evidence levels, Five Element role patterns.

2. **SI-TE Fire channel comparison:** Both Fire element with identical Five Shu roles. SI = Hand Taiyang (ulnar nerve dorsal forearm → suprascapular → cervical → trigeminal), TE = Hand Shaoyang (radial nerve dorsal → posterior brachial cutaneous → cervical → periauricular → trigeminal). Different nerve pathways, same elemental framework.

3. **Periauricular cluster (TE17-TE22):** 6 points around the ear = densest auricular concentration on any channel. Neural anatomy: CN VII exit at TE17 (stylomastoid foramen), great auricular nerve C2-C3 (TE17-TE19), auriculotemporal nerve CN V3 (TE21-TE22). Compare with SI19 and GB2.

4. **Window of Heaven at TE16:** Third Window of Heaven in Atlas (after LI18 anterolateral, SI16 lateral). TE16 = posterolateral position. Completes 3 of ~10 cervical ring points.

5. **Eight Confluent at TE5:** Yang Wei Mai + GB41 (Dai Mai). Compare mechanism with SI3 (Du Mai + BL62 Yang Qiao Mai) and PC6 (Yin Wei Mai + SP4 Chong Mai).

6. **Constipation specificity at TE6:** Among the most condition-specific points in the tradition. Mechanism? Dorsal forearm → posterior interosseous nerve → what pathway to colonic motility?

7. **"No-form organ" paradox:** San Jiao has no Western anatomical correlate. Channel points treat conditions across upper/middle/lower body. Compare with PC (Heart Protector maps to pericardium but traditional functions ≠ pericardial physiology).

8. **Radial nerve territory:** TE forearm points primarily in radial nerve posterior branch territory. Compare with LI (also radial nerve but anterior/lateral branch). Both Yang channels share radial nerve but access different branches.

9. **Moxibustion prohibition at TE23:** Classical moxa prohibition at channel endpoint. Compare with LR10 (五里), LR12 (急脉), LU8 (经渠). Pattern: prohibition sites = proximity to vulnerable structures (eye, major vessels, external genitalia, radial artery).

## Special Considerations

- **Facial nerve at TE17:** Stylomastoid foramen = CN VII exit point. Needling depth critical. HIGH safety concern.
- **Pneumothorax risk at TE15:** Near scapular area, oblique needling toward lung. Assess actual risk.
- **External jugular vein at TE16:** Window of Heaven = cervical zone. Similar vascular risk to SI16, LI18.
- **Orbital risk at TE23:** Lateral eyebrow, near orbital rim. Moxa prohibited (classical) due to eye proximity.
- **Joint capsule at TE14:** Deep needling enters shoulder joint. Risk similar to LI15, SI9.
- **Cross-tradition priorities:** TE5 = strongest trigger point correspondence (common extensor mass). TE14 = infraspinatus/supraspinatus trigger points. TE17 = potential Marma correspondence (Vidhura Marma — behind ear).
