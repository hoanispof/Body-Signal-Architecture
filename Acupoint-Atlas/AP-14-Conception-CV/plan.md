# AP-14 Conception Vessel (CV) — Child Plan

**Meridian:** Nhâm Mạch (任脉, Conception Vessel / Ren Mai) — Extraordinary Vessel with own points
**Points:** 24 (CV1–CV24)
**Paired channel:** NONE (extraordinary vessel, not regular meridian)
**Same-name pair:** NONE
**Phase:** 2 — Systematic by Meridian
**Protocol:** Child Plan Protocol (§10): survey → plan → execute → review
**Date started:** 2026-08-18

## 1. Meridian Profile

**Trajectory:** CV1 (perineum, between anus and external genitalia) → CV2 (superior border of pubic symphysis) → CV3–CV7 (lower abdomen, ascending midline from 4 cun below to 1 cun below umbilicus) → CV8 (umbilicus center) → CV9–CV14 (upper abdomen, 1 cun above umbilicus to xiphoid region) → CV15 (xiphoid process) → CV16–CV21 (sternum, ascending along body/manubrium) → CV22 (suprasternal fossa) → CV23 (above hyoid bone) → CV24 (mentolabial groove, below lower lip).

**Unique features:**
- **ONLY anterior midline meridian** — ALL 24 points are on the midline (midsagittal plane). The Yin counterpart to Du Mai (posterior midline). Together they form the "Small Heavenly Circuit" (小周天, Xiao Zhou Tian) — GV ascending posterior, CV ascending anterior, meeting at perineum (CV1↔GV1) and oral cavity (CV24↔GV28).
- **"Sea of all Yin Meridians" (阴脉之海)** — All Yin channels (LU, SP, HT, KI, PC, LR) connect with CV. CV governs all Yin Qi in the body. Yang counterpart: GV = "Sea of all Yang Meridians" (AP-13).
- **6 FRONT-MU POINTS — most of any channel.** CV3 (Bladder), CV4 (Small Intestine), CV5 (Triple Burner), CV12 (Stomach), CV14 (Heart), CV17 (Pericardium). Half of all 12 Front-Mu points reside on CV. The remaining 6 are: LU1 (Lung, on LU), ST25 (Large Intestine, on ST), LR13 (Spleen, on LR), LR14 (Liver, on LR), GB24 (Gallbladder, on GB), GB25 (Kidney, on GB).
- **2 HUI-MEETING POINTS** — CV12 (Hui-Meeting of Fu Organs, 腑会) and CV17 (Hui-Meeting of Qi, 气会). The other 6 Hui-Meeting are: LR13 (Zang), BL17 (Blood), GB34 (Sinew), LU9 (Vessel), BL11 (Bone), GB39 (Marrow). 2/8 on CV = highest concentration.
- **2 GHOST POINTS** — CV1 (#11 鬼藏 Gui Cang "Ghost Store") and CV24 (#8 鬼市 Gui Shi "Ghost Market"). CV1 in 4th trinity (GV23+CV1+LI11+EX-HN1), CV24 in 3rd trinity (ST6+CV24+PC8). Ghost Points at the Yin axis extremes: perineum (most inferior) and chin (most superior anterior midline face point).
- **Window of Heaven (CV22 Tiantu)** — The LAST Window of Heaven point in the Atlas (10/10). 9 already documented: LU3, LI18, ST9, SI16, SI17, BL10, TE16, GV16, PC1. CV22 completes the Window of Heaven inventory.
- **Luo-Connecting: CV15 (Jiuwei)** — The 14th Luo-Connecting point (12 regular + GV1 + CV15). CV Luo descends from xiphoid, fans across the abdomen. This is the 14th Luo in the Atlas (after 12 regular + GV Luo = AP-13 GV1). Note: GV Luo ascends from coccyx to head (longest Luo), CV Luo disperses across abdomen.
- **CV8 MOXA-ONLY (神阙 Shenque)** — The ONLY point in the entire 361-point system where needling is ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED. Salt-filled moxa (隔盐灸) is the primary technique. Name contains 神 (Spirit). Documented extensively in B08 (lines 319, 441-445, 638, 641, 704).
- **Eight Confluent pair: LU7 (Lieque) + KI6 (Zhaohai)** — Opens Ren Mai + Yin Qiao Mai. Both points documented (AP-01 LU7, AP-08 KI6). LU7+KI6 = most cross-referenced Eight Confluent pair in the Atlas.
- **No Five Shu system** — Ren Mai is an extraordinary vessel, not a principal meridian. Five Shu (Well-Spring-Stream-River-Sea) applies only to the 12 regular meridians.
- **No Chinese clock assignment** — Extraordinary vessels are not part of the 24-hour circadian cycle.
- **KI channel corridor:** KI11–KI21 run 0.5 cun lateral to CV2–CV14 (11 paired points in the abdomen). KI22–KI27 run 2 cun lateral to CV16–CV21 (6 paired points on the chest). Shift from 0.5→2 cun occurs at the thoracoabdominal junction (diaphragm level). Documented extensively in AP-08.
- **Three Wan (三脘) series:** CV10 Xiawan (下脘, Lower), CV12 Zhongwan (中脘, Middle), CV13 Shangwan (上脘, Upper) — three points named for different levels of the stomach cavity. CV12 is the Front-Mu of Stomach, sitting at the midpoint.
- **Cross-channel meetings (documented):** CV+GV+Chong Mai at CV1 (perineum). CV+GV+ST+LI at CV24 (chin). CV+SP+LR+KI at CV3, CV4 (Three Yin meeting). CV+Chong Mai+KI at CV7. CV+ST+SI+TE at CV12, CV13. CV+Yin Wei Mai at CV22, CV23. CV+LR at CV2. CV+SP at CV10.
- **Moxa prohibition:** NONE for CV points except CV8 has the inverse — moxa ONLY, no needling. No other CV point has a moxa prohibition.

**Element:** N/A (extraordinary vessel has no Five Element assignment).

**TCM role:** "Ren" (任) = "responsibility/conception." Ren Mai is responsible for all Yin channels, governs reproduction and conception. Runs through the uterus (classical). Functions: nourishes Yin, regulates menstruation, governs pregnancy and reproductive function, nourishes the throat and chest. Pathology: gynecological disorders, abdominal masses, hernia, leukorrhea, infertility, urogenital dysfunction. Ren Mai is accessed clinically via LU7+KI6, or directly via CV points along the abdomen/chest/throat.

## 6. Architectural Questions to Monitor

1. **FRONT-MU HOSTING PATTERN:** 6/12 Front-Mu points on CV (anterior midline). Only 3 organs have Front-Mu on their OWN channel (LU1, LR14, GB24). Why does the midline host the majority? Is it anatomical — organs project pain/sensitivity to the midline? Or convention — the Ren Mai governs all Yin/internal organs? A07 Beissner data suggests midline Front-Mu DO align with dermatome-viscerotome mapping for some organs (Heart T5, Pericardium T4) but NOT for others (Stomach CV12 vs T6-T10).

2. **SHU-MU ALIGNMENT ASYMMETRY:** Back-Shu (BL, posterior) and Front-Mu (CV, anterior) pairs show mixed segmental alignment. Yin organ pairs (HT: BL15 T5 ↔ CV14 sub-xiphoid, PC: BL14 T4 ↔ CV17 T4 level) match well. Yang organ pairs (ST: BL21 T12 ↔ CV12 epigastric, SI: BL27 S1 ↔ CV4 hypogastric) show the same Dorsher non-matching pattern. Does Front-Mu placement follow organ-body topography while Back-Shu follows segmental innervation?

3. **CV8 PROHIBITION = ANATOMICAL OR CULTURAL?** The umbilicus is the thinnest point of the abdominal wall (no muscle, minimal fascia — only peritoneum, preperitoneal fat, and skin). Needle at CV8 risks direct peritoneal puncture and bowel injury. This is a genuine anatomical hazard, not just tradition. Salt moxa is safe because the salt+moxa combination doesn't penetrate. Is this the best example of traditional safety knowledge encoding genuine anatomical risk?

4. **TWO GHOST POINTS AT YIN EXTREMES:** CV1 (perineum) + CV24 (chin) sit at the absolute bottom and top of the anterior midline. GV has Ghost Points across the cranial-facial axis (posterior). The Ghost Point system may encode a traditional model where "possession/madness" affects the body axis endpoints — the poles where Qi enters/exits the midline vessels. The 3rd trinity (ST6+CV24+PC8) is sometimes called the "addiction/self-harm" trinity.

5. **SMALL HEAVENLY CIRCUIT COMPLETION:** GV28 (upper gum, internal) documents the Du-Ren meeting from the Yang/posterior direction (AP-13). CV24 (chin, external) + CV1 (perineum) document it from the Yin/anterior direction. Qigong tradition uses tongue-palate contact as the "bridge" to close the circuit. Is there a neurological substrate? The tongue tip touching the palate connects lingual nerve (V3) to palatine nerves (V2), creating a trigeminal-mediated anterior-posterior connection.

6. **WINDOW OF HEAVEN CV22 = LOWEST Window of Heaven?** Most Window of Heaven points are at cervical level (C1–C5 region). CV22 at the suprasternal notch sits below C7 = LOWER than all other Window of Heaven points. Does CV22 represent the "root" of the Window of Heaven ring while the others form the "ring" proper? The tracheal/great vessel access at CV22 is unique among Window of Heaven — others access cervical vessels/nerves, but CV22 accesses the mediastinum.

7. **KI-CV CORRIDOR SHIFT:** The 0.5→2 cun lateral shift at diaphragm level (CV14→CV16) mirrors the KI channel's transition from abdominal to thoracic. Why does KI widen from 0.5 to 2 cun? Anatomically: rectus abdominis narrows toward the xiphoid (0.5 cun = on rectus), while the sternum is wider (2 cun = lateral to sternum on costal cartilage). The shift marks the transition from muscular abdominal wall to rigid bony thorax.

8. **THREE WAN GASTRIC MAPPING:** CV10 (Lower Wan), CV12 (Middle Wan), CV13 (Upper Wan) — named for stomach cavity levels. CV12 is 4 cun above umbilicus (approximately at the pylorus/antrum), CV10 at 2 cun (below stomach), CV13 at 5 cun (approximately at cardia/fundus). Do these actually map to gastric anatomy? The names suggest traditional recognition of the stomach's vertical anatomy.

9. **SPIRIT NAME ON CV8:** CV8 (神阙 Shenque, "Spirit Gate") contains 神 (Spirit). Compare: GV10 (靈臺 Spirit Tower), GV11 (神道 Spirit Path), GV24 (神庭 Spirit Court). KI23-25 have Spirit names. CV8 = the only Spirit-named point that is moxa-only. Is there a pattern — Spirit names at neurologically "deep" access points? The navel = embryonic connection, psychologically significant.

10. **CV4 + GV4 = GATE PAIR?** CV4 (关元 Guanyuan, "Gate of Origin") and GV4 (命门 Mingmen, "Gate of Life") sit on OPPOSITE SIDES of the body at approximately the same vertebral level (CV4 at 3 cun below umbilicus ≈ L2 level, GV4 at L2 spinous process). Both are major tonification points with moxa as primary technique. Both govern "Kidney Yang." The anterior-posterior "Gate" pairing at L2 — where the renal arteries, adrenal glands, and lumbar splanchnic nerves converge — may represent traditional recognition of the L2 adrenal-renal axis.
